How you progress on the portal: XP and levels, Coins and Contribution, streaks, achievements, quests, games and bonus campaigns.

## Your three values

![My progression: the level, the three values, where the XP comes from, the streak and the gains, numbered](shot:progression-apercu)

1. **Your level** and its bar; below it, the XP you still need for the next one.
2. **Your three values**: XP, Contribution and Coins.
3. **« Where my XP comes from »**: the view (« By source », « Over time », « Total XP »), the value shown and the period.
4. **Your current streak**, if streaks are on.
5. **Your gains** by source and by day.

On [My progression](/progression), you see three separate values:

| Value | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **XP** | Raises your level. |
| **Coins** | The portal's currency, to spend in [the shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique). Its name may differ on your portal. |
| **Contribution** | Reflects what you bring to the community. Some products ask for a minimum Contribution. |

These values are never converted into one another.

## Earning XP and Coins

What earns, and how much, depends on your portal. Depending on its settings, it can be:
- watching a film or an episode to the end, finishing a season, listening, reading;
- watching live TV: the time spent in front of each programme;
- playing a game from the library (RomM): the time spent playing, and a big reward once you have played a game for as long as its estimated length (its main story, when known);
- making a request, or seeing a request become available;
- signing in to the portal;
- playing the [games](/games), completing a challenge of the week, completing a line or the full card of the month's bingo;
- sending feedback or opening a support ticket, reporting a problem with a media item (once the team has fixed it);
- giving your opinion on a title you watched, read or played (once per title);
- completing a quest, unlocking an achievement, reaching a level.

Some actions have a cap, or a delay between two gains.

> [!NOTE]
> When the media server knows where the end credits start, a film or an episode stopped in the **last two minutes** before those credits counts as finished: no need to sit through the credits. Otherwise, the share you watched is what counts. For a game, only the time played counts: your sessions come up from RomM a few minutes after you finish them, and a game marked « finished » in RomM earns nothing more. A season counts as finished when all its episodes are watched, on the date of the last one, whatever order you watched them in.

To see where your XP comes from, open « Where my XP comes from » on [My progression](/progression). Choose a period: the chart shows your gains by source (films, episodes, quests, streak bonus…). « My plays by kind » details your sittings and their duration. « History » lists every operation (gain, spending, refund) with your balance right after.

## Levelling up

- The level bar shows how much XP you still need (« … XP to level … »).
- When you level up, a « Level …! » window opens with « What you won ».
- Depending on your portal, a level can bring Coins, Contribution, a little XP or a free product (for example a booster to open from [My reserve](/marketplace/reserve)).
- Some shop products ask for a minimum level. When you reach it, the window says so: « Now in the shop: … ».

## Keeping a streak

If the administrator has turned it on, the **streak** counts your consecutive days of activity. By default, that means finishing a film or an episode, after at least a few minutes of playback.

- The longer the streak, the more the gains of those actions are multiplied. On [My progression](/progression), « Current streak » shows your days in a row and the multiplier, then « … more days to reach ×… ».
- Miss a day, and the streak starts over from zero.
- Days follow the portal's time zone.

> [!TIP]
> A **streak freeze**, bought in the shop or won in a booster, automatically covers a missed day. The streak and its multiplier carry on. Your freezes are shown on your progression.

## Making the most of a boost

An **XP boost** from the shop multiplies your gains for a while. The running boost shows its multiplier and when it ends (« Boost ×… — ends … »). Boosts, streaks and campaigns add up, within the limit set by your portal.

## Unlocking achievements

An achievement unlocks only once, when all its conditions are met: watching a number of films, reaching a level…

Find them on [My progression](/progression) or on the [Achievements](/community/achievements) page, in your personal menu:

![Achievements: your progress (1), the categories and the sorting (2), an achievement (3)](shot:progression-succes)

1. Your progress: how many achievements you have unlocked out of all the portal's.
2. The categories and « Sort by ».
3. An achievement: what it asks for, what it gives, its rarity and the share of members who have it.

In detail:
- grouped into « Unlocked », « In progress » and « The others », with your progress (for example 3 / 10);
- filtered by category and sorted by « Rarity », « Recently unlocked » or « Name »;
- with the share of members who have it. The fewer they are, the rarer the achievement.

A **hidden achievement** reveals nothing until it is unlocked. An achievement can give XP, Coins, Contribution or a free product (« What it gives »).

## Completing quests

The [Quests](/games/quests) page, in the **Games** section, lists all your quests. [My progression](/progression) and the Community's « Overview » tab show some too.

![The Quests page: the filters (1), your progress (2), a quest (3) and its time left (4)](shot:progression-quetes)

1. **The filters**: « All », « Today », « This week », « This month », « Quests » and « Challenges ». Each shows how many are left to do. The portal remembers your choice.
2. **« Quests done »**: how many you have done out of the total, with its bar.
3. **A quest**: its kind, what it asks for, your progress and its reward (for example « +25 XP »).
4. **Its time left**: « … left », or « Until … ».

There are several kinds of quests:

- **Daily objective**, **Weekly objective** or **Monthly objective**: comes back every period.
- **Quest**: one or more steps to complete.
- **Challenge**: limited in time.
- **Discovery**: a mission to discover a part of the portal.

Objectives can be about any part of the portal, depending on your portal: signing in, watching, reading or listening, making a request, playing the games, writing to support…

A completed quest is marked « Done », and its reward is credited to you. Completed quests are kept apart, below the ones still to do. An objective that comes back says when: « Back in … » or « Back on … ».

**First steps**, discovery missions for new members, show on your home page. Each one is rewarded only once. See [First steps](portalab://wiki/guide-premiers-pas).

## Games

The **Games** section of the menu brings together « Today's games », « Quests », « Play together » and « Mediacards » (see [Mediacards](portalab://wiki/guide-cartes)). The games are « drawn from the household's library: alone, every day, or together ». What you see there follows your play level, set in [My settings → Play](/settings/play): « Today's games » and « Play together » at « Keen », « Mediacards » from « Collector », « Quests » from « Passive »; at « Just the media », none of these pages.

### Today's games

![Today's games: the wheel (1), the title of the day (2), the quests reminder (3), this week's challenges (4) and a game (5)](shot:progression-jeux)

1. **« The daily wheel »**: spin it once a day. It can give Coins, XP, a booster…
2. **« Title of the day »**: the day's title to find letter by letter, the same for everyone. « Share my result » copies your grid.
3. **« Quests done »**: where your quests stand, how many are left to do today and the next one. « See the quests » opens the quests page. A running campaign has its own reminder next to it.
4. **« This week's challenges »**: a game to play, a film to watch through, a book and an audiobook to get on with. Your services and the portal's player count your progress on their own: nothing to declare.
5. **« All the games »**: each game shows whether it is played « Alone » or « Together », and how many games you have left today (« … left today »), depending on your portal.

**« The … bingo »** (the current month), above the challenges: one card for the whole month, the same for every member, with squares such as « Some horror », « A title from the 80s », « Made in Japan », « A series' very first episode », « 30 min of live TV » or « Review a title ». A square ticks itself when you finish a title that fits it (a film or an episode watched to the end; a book, an audiobook or a game a quarter of an hour at a time; live TV or the radio half an hour over the month), stamped with the title's poster. A line (a row, a column or a diagonal) and the full card earn rewards — the full card may also give a Mediacards booster of the card's genre (or its era); a new card arrives on the 1st of the month.

Depending on your portal, you will find for example « The blurred scene », « The hidden synopsis », « The cast », « Higher or lower », « Timeline », « Blind test », « Memories » or « Memory ». Tap a game, then « Start ». At the bottom of the page, the **« Boards »** show the best scores of each game (« Board of … »).

### Play together

« Open a party, share its code, and play each on your phone, the party's screen on the television. »

1. On [Play together](/games/party), under « Open a party », choose the « Game ». « I play too » is ticked: untick it to only host the party (its screen on the TV, for example). Tap « Open a party ».
2. Give the code to the other players, or « Copy the link ».
3. The others enter the « Party code » on « Today's games » or on « Play together », then « Join ».
4. When everyone is there, tap « Start the party ».

The « Who watched it? » game can only be played together, and only among players who agreed to it in their settings.

## Bonus campaigns

A **campaign** is a special period when some actions earn more. Open [Campaigns](/campaigns) to see the ones running.

A campaign can bring:
- a **bonus**, for example ×2 XP on films of a genre, or when you finish an episode;
- **discounts** on some shop products;
- **special quests** and **special achievements**.

A campaign's page shows « What it brings you », when it ends and its « Next periods ». A notification can also tell you when a campaign starts.

## Your place in the leaderboards

[My progression](/progression) also shows your position in the leaderboards. The full leaderboards are in the Community section, « Leaderboards » tab: see [Community](portalab://wiki/guide-communaute).
