Find a title in the library, read its page, and watch it, read it or listen to it without leaving the portal.

## The Media page

Open the **Media** section of the menu: its tabs are « Library », « Request a title », « My requests » and « My taste ». The « Library », the media home page, offers you:

- **« Browse the catalog »** to search the whole library;
- **« Request a title »** to ask for a title that is missing (see [Request a title](portalab://wiki/guide-demandes));
- what is **« Now playing »** for you right now;
- rows you scroll sideways: **« Continue »**, **« Recently added »**, **« Recent releases »**, **« Leaving soon »**, **« Recently played »**, **« Trending »**, **« Recommended for you »**;
- **« Browse by kind »**: films, series, books, audiobooks, music, games…

![The Media page: « Browse the catalog », « Request a title » and a row of titles](shot:medias-accueil)

A row with nothing to show is not displayed. If your portal has a radio, it appears here too: **« Listen »** to listen live, and **« Ask the DJ »** to suggest a track.

## Finding a title in the catalog

Open [the catalog](/media/browse), then:

![The catalog: the search (1), the kind (2), the filters (3) and the sort order (4)](shot:medias-catalogue)

1. Type in **« Search titles »**: a title, an author, an actor…
2. Choose a **« Kind »**: « Everything », « Films », « Series », « Books », « Audiobooks », « Music » or « Games ».
3. Narrow it down with **« Filters »**: « Availability », « Already seen » (« Finished », « In progress », « Not yet »), « Genre », « Year » (from… to…), and depending on the kind « Author », « Director », « Narrator », « Series », « Publisher »…
4. Order the results with **« Sort by »**: « Recently added », « Title (A to Z) » or « Newest ».

Active filters are shown above the results. Remove one with a click, or click **« Clear all »**. The **« Show more »** button loads the next titles.

## A title's page

Click a poster to open the title's page. At the top, you find the poster, the year, the kind, the length and the rating. Right next to them, badges:

- **« Available »** when the title is in the library. Otherwise, the stage its request has reached (for example « Waiting for approval » or « Downloading 40% »), or **« Not available »**;
- **« In progress · 35 % »** if you have started it (or the current episode, for a series);
- **« Watched »**, **« Read »**, **« Listened »** or **« Played »** if you have finished it.

> [!TIP]
> No need to sit through all the credits: when the media server knows where they start, a film or an episode stopped within the last two minutes before the end credits counts as finished, for your statistics as well as your rewards. A season is finished when all its episodes have been watched, on the date of the last one.

![The top of a film's page: its badges, and the buttons to watch it](shot:medias-fiche)

Under the title, a bar of small actions:

- the three taste icons: **« I like it »**, **« Not for me »**, **« Later »** (their names show when you hover over them);
- **« Give my review »**, once you have watched, read, listened to or played the title: a window asks for a rating from 1 to 5 stars (each one says what it means, from « Didn't like it » to « Loved it! ») and **« A word on this title »** if you like, then **« Post my review »**. The button then shows your rating (« My review: 4/5 ») and reopens the window so you can edit or remove it. A review of an episode goes to its series;
- **« Follow »** for a series;
- the **« Report a problem »** flag: missing subtitles, wrong language, a file that cuts off, a picture issue? Choose what is wrong (« Subtitles », « Sound », « Picture », « Language », « File unreadable or cut », « Something else »), add details if needed, then **« Send the report »**. It becomes a support ticket linked to the title, which you follow in [My tickets](/support); the team sees the title in the ticket.

A title with open reports carries a small beating orange flag on its poster, wherever it appears (library, shelves, home widgets, search), with how many there are if there is more than one. On its page, an **« N open reports »** badge beats in its header; it leads to **« Reported problems »**, at the bottom of the page: yours (tap them to follow them) and, if your portal shares them, the other members' ones — only their title and state, never who made them — so that a known problem is not reported twice. **« Show the closed reports too »** shows the ones that have been fixed.

Further down, **« Members' reviews »** gives the average rating and the number of reviews, then the reviews themselves: yours first, the others signed with their name, or **« Anonymous »** for a member who stays discreet in the community. The section only appears once a first review has been given.

Further down, depending on the title:

- **« Enriched details »**: cast, crew, genres, budget and box office, studios… Click a person to open their page: their biography and filmography, with what is available and what you can request. For an author, when the portal is connected to Hardcover, their books are added, the most read first, each one **« Available »** or **« Not here »** (books cannot be requested); the list is then called **« Works »** (**« Bibliography »** if they only wrote books), next to the films and series they took part in;
- **« Videos »**: trailers, teasers, featurettes, behind the scenes, clips… and a game's gameplay videos, the trailers in your language first. With several kinds, buttons (**« All »**, **« Trailer »**, **« Behind the scenes »**…) keep just one. A media server's video plays in the portal's player; a YouTube video plays in a window of the portal, or opens on YouTube when the portal is reached by its IP address (YouTube then refuses to play anywhere else);
- **« Pictures »**: backdrops, stills of the film or series, a game's screenshots and artworks. Tap one to see it large: **« Next picture »** and **« Previous picture »**, the arrow keys or a swipe to move between them, Escape or the cross to go back;
- the title's **collection**, presented the same way for a film (**« Saga »**), a book or an audiobook (**« Series »**) and a game (**« Franchise »**): its name, the title's place in it (« Volume 2 »), how many of its titles are in the library and their covers to scroll through. **« See the saga »** (or the series, the franchise) opens its page with all its titles in order; for a saga, it also shows the missing films, with **« Ask for it »**, and for a book series all its volumes (when the portal is connected to Hardcover), the missing ones marked **« Not here »**;
- **« About »**, **« Sources »** (where the title can be found), **« Requests »** and **« External links »**;
- for a game, **« Time to beat »**: how long it takes depending on how you play (« Main story », « Main + extras », « Completionist », « All styles »), with how many players reported it (from HowLongToBeat, when RomM knows the game);
- **« My history »**: each time you played this title, the date and how far you got. Once enough members have seen it, the number of plays and members is shown too, without any names.

If the title is not here yet, a **« Request »** button appears instead. If a title may be deleted, a **« Leaving soon »** box warns you (see [Titles leaving soon](portalab://wiki/guide-bientot-supprime)).

## Watching a film or a series

On the page of an available title:

- **« Watch »** opens the portal's player in full screen. For a series, the button tells you the episode, for example « Watch S01E03 ».
- **« Resume »** picks up where you left off. Next to it, two small buttons: **« Start over »** (from the beginning) and **« Back to zero »** (clears your resume point).
- **« Watch on … »**, followed by the name of your media server (for example « Watch on Plex »), opens the title in its app, in a new tab.

In the player, the **« Playback settings »** button lets you choose:

- the **« Quality »**: « Original » plays the file as it is if your browser can read it; a lower quality is converted by the server;
- the **« Version »**, the **« Audio »** track, the **« Subtitles »** and their **« Subtitle size »**;
- the **« Audio boost »** and the **« Speed »**;
- the **« Chapters »**.

You can also **« Skip intro »** and **« Skip credits »**. At the end of an episode, **« Up next »** starts the next one, unless you choose **« Watch the credits »**. Depending on your device, the player offers **« Picture in picture »**, **« Cast to a device »** or **« AirPlay »**.

> [!TIP]
> Player keyboard shortcuts: **Space** or **K** to play and pause, **←** and **→** to go back or forward 10 seconds, **↑** and **↓** for the volume, **M** to mute, **F** for full screen, **C** for subtitles, **Esc** to leave.

> [!NOTE]
> Depending on your portal, you may need to link your media server account to watch here. The player tells you so (« Link your media server account to watch here ») and takes you to your media accounts.

## Reading a book

On a book's page, **« Read »** opens the reader in full screen. If you have already started it, the button becomes **« Resume reading (42%) »**. The reader opens EPUB, PDF and comic book formats.

In it, you will find:

- the **« Contents »**, the **« Bookmarks »** (« Bookmark this page ») and **« Search the book »**;
- the **« Reading settings »**: « Font » (including « Easy reading »), text size, « Line spacing », « Margins », « Colours » (« Light », « Sepia », « Dark » or « Follow the theme »), « Continuous scrolling », « Two pages side by side », and « Right to left (manga) » for comics;
- **« Back to the title »** to return to the title's page.

The **« Read on… »** button also opens the book in the library's own service.

## Listening to an audiobook

On an audiobook's page, **« Listen »** (or **« Resume »**) starts playback in a bar at the bottom of the screen. It keeps playing while you move around the portal.

**« Expand the player »** shows the cover and more controls:

- go back or forward 30 seconds, **« Chapters »**, previous or next chapter;
- the **« Speed »**;
- a **« Sleep timer »**: 10 to 60 minutes, or **« End of the chapter »**;
- **« Bookmarks »** (« Add a bookmark »).

> [!NOTE]
> Your position is always kept in the portal. If the library's service does not know it, link your account to sync it.

## My taste

[My taste](/media/taste), in the Media section: « What you watch, listen to, read and play, and what you say of it, makes your recommendations. »

- **« Tell what you like »**: titles to judge, one after another. Swipe right for « I like it », left for « Not for me », or « Skip ».
- **« For you »**: your recommendations, by « Kind of title ».
- **« Something different »**: « Well rated, off your usual paths — and maybe for you. »
- **« What you like »**: the genres, people, sagas and themes that keep coming up in your taste.
- **« My lists »**: « Liked », « Not for me » and « For later ».
- **« My data »**: « Use my history » (when unticked, only your verdicts count) and « Forget my verdicts ».

## Linking your media accounts

Open [Media accounts](/settings/media-accounts) (in [My settings](/settings), « Linked accounts »). Linking your accounts lets the portal know that your activity on those services is yours: history, resume points, statistics, recommendations and, depending on your portal, the player.

1. In **« Link an account »**, choose the service.
2. Enter the name or number of your account on that service, then click **« Link account »**.
3. The account shows as **« Waiting for verification »**. If the service gave you a **« Verification code »**, enter it and click **« Verify »**. Otherwise, an administrator confirms the link.

Nothing is credited to you until the link is **« Verified »**. Your administrator may already have linked your accounts: they then appear in **« Linked accounts »**. To remove an account, click **« Remove »**.
