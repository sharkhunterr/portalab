A film or a series is missing from the library? Request it, see what it takes from your quota, and follow the request until the title is available.

## Finding a title to request

Open [Request a title](/media/discover) in the « Media » section of the menu, or click **« Request a title »** from the Media page.

- Type in **« A film, a series, a book, a game… »**: you also find titles that are not in the library.
- Or browse the rows: **« Trending »**, **« Popular films »**, **« Popular series »**, **« Upcoming films »**, **« Upcoming series »**.

Each poster carries a badge: **« To ask for »**, **« Requested »**, **« You asked for it »**, **« Already requested »**, **« In progress »**, **« Partly available »** or **« Available »**.

![The Request a title page: the search, and each poster's badge](shot:demandes-chercher)

Click a poster to see its page: synopsis, genres, length, rating, cast, **« Trailer »** and, for a series, its seasons. If the title is already here, **« See it in the library »** takes you there.

> [!TIP]
> On an actor's, a director's or an author's page, the **« To ask for »** filter shows what is missing from their filmography. Each missing title has its own **« Ask for it »** button.

## Making a request

1. On the title's page, click **« Ask for it »**.
2. For a series, choose the **« Seasons »**: every missing season is ticked by default. Seasons already here or already requested cannot be ticked.
3. If your portal offers it, tick **« In 4K »**.
4. Read the quota line under the form: it says how many requests you have left, or whether this request will use a request you bought.
5. Click **« Ask for it »**. The message « Asked! » confirms it was sent. **« See my requests »** opens the list of your requests.

![The request form for a series: its seasons, the quota line and the « Ask for it » button](shot:demandes-formulaire)

You can also request from the page of a library title that is not available yet: there, the **« Request »** button takes the place of the « Watch » button.

> [!NOTE]
> A film another member has already requested cannot be requested again. It will arrive for everyone: follow its progress on its page. If the request service refuses (for example, 4K or this kind of title is not allowed for your account), the portal tells you and nothing is counted.

## Your request quota

The number of requests you can make is limited. The limits depend on your portal:

- if your account on the request service is linked, **its** limits are the ones that count: a number of films and a number of series, separately, every so many days. The portal shows the date when a request comes back;
- otherwise, the portal gives you a number of requests for the day, the week or the month.

Your quota shows in **« My request quota »** on the Request a title page, and at the top of your requests in figures ([My requests](/media/requests/stats), Statistics section).

**When your quota is reached**, you can buy extra requests in the shop, with the portal's currency (Coins): follow the **« More requests in the shop »** link. They are only used once your quota is used up, and stay yours until you use them.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A request you bought is given back to you if your request is declined, if the request service refuses it, or if the service does not answer.

## Following your requests

Open [My requests](/media/requests), in the « Media » section. The page has two tabs:

- **« List »**: all your requests, with a search and the filters **« Pending »**, **« In progress »**, **« Available »** and **« Declined »**. Each line shows its stage: « Waiting for approval », « Out on… », « Looking for a release », « Downloading 60% », « Importing », « Available »…
- **« Coming soon »**: what is downloading, being imported or has a release date.

### Your requests in figures

Your figures are in the **Statistics** section, on the [My requests](/media/requests/stats) page.

![My requests in figures: the period (1) and the summary of your requests (2)](shot:demandes-mes-demandes)

1. The **« Period »**.
2. The summary of your requests over that period.

Further down: **« My requests' status »**, **« What became of them »**, **« My rank as a requester »** (anonymous), **« Requests over time »**, **« Time to availability »**, the most watched titles among those requested, and the genres you request against the genres you watch. Some of these cards are unlocked in the shop, depending on your portal. See [My statistics and my recap](portalab://wiki/guide-statistiques).

**Cancelling a request**: as long as it is pending, click **« Cancel »** in the list, or **« Cancel the request »** in the « Requests » section of the title's page.

If an administrator declines your request, a notification tells you, with their reason if they gave one. The reason also shows on the title's page.

## A title's journey

On the page of a requested title, the **« Where this title stands »** box shows its journey, step by step:

1. **« Requested »**: by you or by a member, and when.
2. **« Approved »**: depending on your portal, an administrator approves the request.
3. **« Released »**: a film still in cinemas has to wait for its digital release. The release dates (cinema, digital, Blu-ray/DVD) are shown.
4. **« Searched »**: the download manager looks for a release.
5. **« Downloading »**: the progress, the speed and the time left.
6. **« Imported »**: the file enters the library.
7. **« Available »**: the title is ready.

Each step is « done », « in progress », « waiting », « blocked » (someone has to step in), « failed » or « to come ». The current step is explained just below the timeline. Expand **« Every step »** to read the details of each one.

![The « Where this title stands » box of a requested title that has not arrived yet](shot:demandes-parcours)

In the picture: the timeline of steps (1), the current step explained (2), **« Every step »** (3) and **« Ask the assistant »** (4).

A question? If the assistant is available on your portal, click **« Ask the assistant »**: it explains where the title stands and why it is not available yet.

Once the title has arrived, the journey folds into one line (« Available · since… »). Click **« See the journey »** to open it again.

> [!NOTE]
> If you have unlocked this feature (for example in the shop, depending on your portal), the **« See the indexers' results »** button shows the releases found right now: their quality, their sources, and whether they are accepted or rejected. The search can take a minute. Its result is kept for 30 minutes.

## Requesting a book, a game…

Films and series go through the request service; a book, an audiobook or a game, if your portal offers it, goes through the team. For you, it is the same request: it has the same steps, counts in the same quota and is followed in the same place.

It all starts from [Request a title](/media/discover): type a film, a series, a book or a game. Below the films and series, the **« Books, audiobooks and games »** section lists those the library does not have; each one opens its page. Cannot find it? **« Ask the team »**, below the results, opens the form. The search's **« Ask for it »** section also offers these books and games. On an author's page or a book series' page, a missing book has its **« Ask for it »** button. A book the library only has as an audiobook has the **« Ask for the book »** button, which opens its page.

The **page** of a missing book is laid out like that of a book in the library: authors, series, genres, rating, summary, and its **« Editions »**, those in your language first (translated title, format, audiobook, publisher, ISBN). If the library already has the book, its own page opens. If the library only has it as an audiobook, the page says so (**« As an audiobook in the library »**) and the book can still be requested. For a game, when the portal has a games database (IGDB), a franchise's page lists all its games. A missing game has its own page too: summary, platforms, studios, genres, rating, trailer, screenshots.

**« Ask for it »** opens the form in a window, already filled in. From an edition's or a platform's line, its title, language, format and ISBN go along with the request:

1. Check the **« Kind »** and the **« Title »**.
2. The **« Author, studio or creator »** helps find the right title. Several? Separate them with a comma.
3. If you like, add the **« Year »**, the **« Language »**, the **« Edition »** (a format, a platform, a publisher…) and **« A word for the team »**.
4. Click **« Send the request »**.

The portal tells you straight away if the library already has this title (the **« See it »** link takes you there), or if it has already been requested: like a film, it will arrive for everyone.

Your request joins [My requests](/media/requests), with your films and series. The team accepts or declines it (with its reason). It closes on its own when the title arrives in the library, and you are told, as for a film.

## Getting notified

You get a notification when your request is accepted or declined, or when the title becomes available. Set them up in your notification preferences (« My media requests » and « Requested media available » categories): see [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications).

On your home page, the **« My requests »**, **« My requests coming soon »** and **« Next releases »** widgets also follow your requests.
