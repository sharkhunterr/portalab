Your home page, the dashboard: what it shows, how to customize it, add widgets and change how they look.

## What the home page shows

The home page greets you by name (« Good morning », « Good afternoon », « Good evening »), with today's date. Below, your **dashboard** is made of **widgets**: blocks that each show one thing, for example what you are watching, the latest additions to the library, your progression or the next releases.

If you are a new member, the « First steps » card also offers a few guided missions (see [First steps](portalab://wiki/guide-premiers-pas)).

The « Since your last visit » widget sums up what changed for you: a request now available, a reply on a ticket, new things in the library or in the shop, what you earned… « Mark as seen » or « Mark all as seen » clears it.

> [!NOTE]
> Your first dashboard is the one your administrator prepared for your play profile, built from what you ticked on the « Your portal, to your taste » welcome screen. You can then change it as you like, except the widgets they made required. If you change your play profile, « Take my profile's dashboard », in [My settings → Play](/settings/play), gives you the one of your new profile.

## Customize the dashboard

1. On the home page, click « Customize ».
2. Arrange your widgets:
   - grab a widget by its **handle** to move it;
   - drag its **corner** or its edges to make it bigger or smaller;
   - with the keyboard: **Enter** grabs the widget, the **arrow keys** move it, **Shift + arrows** resize it, **Enter** drops it.
3. Click « Save » when you are happy with it, or « Cancel » to put everything back as it was.

![The dashboard in « Customize » mode, with its eight numbered markers](shot:accueil-personnaliser)

1. « Add a widget » opens the catalog.
2. « Save » keeps your changes; « Cancel », right next to it, drops them.
3. The « Computer », « Tablet » and « Phone » screens: the one you are arranging.
4. « One layout per screen » (see below).
5. « Auto fit »; « Default » is right next to it.
6. A widget's « Settings » (gear) and « Remove » (bin) buttons.
7. The widget's **handle**, to move it.
8. The widget's **corner**, to make it bigger or smaller.

While you customize, the « Layout » bar also offers:

- « Auto fit »: fills the gaps between widgets;
- « Default »: goes back to the starting dashboard, the one of your play profile. The portal asks you to confirm, because your widgets and their layout are replaced.

### One layout per screen

By default, your dashboard has **a single layout**: tablet and phone follow the computer's. Turn on « One layout per screen » to arrange « Computer », « Tablet » and « Phone » each on its own.

If you arrange your dashboard from a phone or a tablet while the layout is shared, that screen gets its own layout when you save; the others keep theirs.

> [!TIP]
> If your dashboard changed on another device while you were arranging it, the portal reloads it and tells you.

## Add a widget

1. Click « Customize », then « Add a widget ».
2. The « Widget catalog » opens. Use « Search a widget » to filter it.
3. Each widget shows a preview, what it does, its possible sizes and what it reads of your data (for example « Reads: your progression »).
4. Click « Add ». A widget already placed shows « Added »; some can be added several times (« Add again »).
5. Click « Done », then « Save ».

![The widget catalog: the search, the widgets from the shop, then for each widget its preview, what it does and the « Add » button](shot:accueil-catalogue)

The catalog keeps the widgets « To add » apart from those « Already on your dashboard ». It also tells you how many more widgets are available in the shop.

### A few widgets

The catalog only shows the widgets you can use. Depending on your portal, you will find for example:

| Theme | Examples |
|---|---|
| Media | « Continue », « Recently added », « Next releases », « My requests », « Leaving soon », « Shuffle », « Poster wall » |
| Progression | « My progression », « Daily objectives », « Wheel of fortune », « My Mediacards », « Progress bar », « Value » |
| Shop | « Marketplace », « My reserve » |
| Community | « Community activity », « Leaderboard », « Community projects » |
| News | « My feed », « Latest news », « Featured news », « Calendar », « Upcoming », « Open incidents » |
| Practical | « Quick access », « Clock », « Note », « Countdown », « Shortcut », « Separator », « My tickets » |

### The « Quick access » widget

« Quick access » shows the portal's services, their state and a direct link to each one.

![The « Quick access » widget: a service (1) and the colour of its state (2)](shot:accueil-acces-rapides)

1. **A service**: tap it to open it.
2. **Its state**, in colour.

The colour follows the service's state: « Operational », « Degraded », « Maintenance », « Unavailable »… The widget adapts to its size: tiles in a large block, rows in a narrow column, pills on a single row.

In its « Settings », tick the services to show and move them by their handle (or with the arrow keys) to choose their order. If you choose none, « Every service, in the usual order » is shown, including the ones added later.

### The « My feed » widget

« My feed » shows the latest news from your sources: articles, videos, podcasts. YouTube videos play right in the widget, and « All my feed » opens the page. See [News, calendar and wiki](portalab://wiki/guide-actualites).

### The « My reserve » and « My Mediacards » widgets

« My reserve » counts what is waiting in your reserve: « To complete », « To open », « To use », « Waiting for their level ». It shows the first items (identical items stacked, with their number) and how many arrived since your last visit. « My reserve », at the top of the widget, opens the page. See [My reserve](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique).

« My Mediacards » shows where your collection stands: the cards you have out of all the cards there are, the sets you started, your copies. Your boosters to open pile up there as they do on the page, and lead to its « To open » tab. If you do not collect Mediacards, the widget stays empty. See [The Mediacards](portalab://wiki/guide-cartes).

On a single row, or in a low block, both widgets only show their figures.

## Set up a widget

In « Customize » mode, each widget has two small buttons:

- the **gear**, « Settings », opens its options;
- the **bin**, « Remove », takes it off the dashboard.

The options depend on the widget: the period, the kind of media, « Whose » (« Mine » or « Everyone’s »), the text of a note, the date of a countdown… Every widget also has « Display » options:

- « Hide the title »: the content alone, without its heading;
- « Without its box »: no background and no border;
- « Hide when there is nothing to show »: the block disappears while it is empty and comes back as soon as it has content.

![A widget's settings: an option of its own (1), its « Visual » (2) and the « Display » part shared by every widget (3)](shot:accueil-reglages)

When a widget has more items than it can show, it offers « See all », or suggests you make it bigger.

## Change how a widget looks

Many widgets can change their look without changing their content. In its « Settings », choose another « Visual », for example « Classic », « Carousel », « Card deck », « Flip board », « Counting figures », « Analog face » or « Liquid gauges », depending on the widget.

A visual marked with a padlock and « in the shop » is not yours yet: you can get it in the [shop](/marketplace). If you own visual packs, the « Pack look » option also offers their frames. See [The shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique).

## Required widgets

Your administrator can set some widgets for everyone:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| « Pinned » | Placed here by your administrator: you cannot move it or remove it |
| « Required by your administrator » | Always there, but you can move it |
| « Locked by your administrator » | Its size and settings are fixed |

> [!NOTE]
> If a widget shows « A source did not answer », a service is briefly unreachable: the rest of your dashboard works normally.
