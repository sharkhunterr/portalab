Quick answers to the most common questions. Each answer points to the guide page that tells you more.

## Getting started

**How do I search for something quickly?**
Press Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) or « / » from any page, type a few letters, then Enter. « Search for “…” » opens all the results with filters; before you type, « Advanced search » opens the search page. See [First steps](portalab://wiki/guide-premiers-pas).

**Where can I see a guided tour again?**
On the [Guided tours](/guide) page (« Guide », in the Help section): « Start » begins a tour, « See again » plays it once more.

**How do I change the widgets on my home page?**
Click « Customize », then « Add a widget » to add one, the gear to set one up, the bin to remove one. Finish with « Save ». See [The home page and its widgets](portalab://wiki/guide-accueil).

**Why can't I remove or move a widget?**
Your administrator made it required or locked it: the widget says so (« Pinned », « Required by your administrator », « Locked by your administrator »).

## Control center and notifications

**What does the icon (heart, wrench, warning sign) in the header mean?**
It is the control center. The heart means everything is fine; the other icons point to an incident, a maintenance, services in trouble or an announcement. Click it for the details. See [Control center](portalab://wiki/guide-centre-de-controle).

**I closed an announcement. How do I read it again?**
Open the control center: current announcements stay there, even closed ones. Some announcements cannot be closed, by your administrator's choice.

**How do I get fewer e-mails?**
In your notification preferences, turn off « By e-mail » on the lines you want, or choose « Daily digest » or « Weekly digest ». You unsubscribe from newsletters one by one in « Newsletters by e-mail », or with « Unsubscribe from this newsletter » at the bottom of the e-mail. See [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications).

## Media and requests

**How do I ask for a film that is not in the library?**
Open « Request a title » in the Media section, search for the title, open its page and click « Request ». For a series, choose the seasons. See [Request a title](portalab://wiki/guide-demandes).

**I have no requests left. What can I do?**
Wait for a request to come back (the date shows in « My request quota »), or buy extra requests in the shop. They are only used once your quota runs out.

**My request was declined: do I lose the request I bought?**
No. A bought request is given back to you if the request is declined, if the service declines it or if it does not answer.

**How do I hear about what is coming for an actor or a series?**
Tap « Follow » on their page: what comes up for them appears in [My feed](/feed), and « For me » keeps only what concerns you. See [News, calendar and wiki](portalab://wiki/guide-actualites).

**I stopped a film during the credits: does it count as watched?**
Yes, if you stopped it within the last two minutes before the end credits (when the media server knows where they start). See [Progress, quests and games](portalab://wiki/guide-progression).

**Where does the title I asked for stand?**
On its page, the « Where this title stands » box shows each step, from « Requested » to « Available », and « Ask the assistant » explains it to you. The full list is in « My requests », in the Media section.

**A title I like is going to be deleted. Can I keep it?**
Yes. On its page, click « Save this title » in the « Leaving soon » box. You need a rescue, bought in the shop (« Rescue » products) or won in a booster. The title is saved for everyone, for good. See [Titles leaving soon](portalab://wiki/guide-bientot-supprime).

**Why does the player ask me to link my account?**
Depending on your portal, the player needs your media server account. Link it in « Media accounts »: your progress and your history stay yours. See [My account and my settings](portalab://wiki/guide-compte).

**Why are my statistics empty?**
They appear once a library service reports what you play and your media accounts are linked and verified. See [My statistics and my recap](portalab://wiki/guide-statistiques).

**Where do I find my recap?**
A « Your … recap is ready » notification tells you at the end of the period. It opens the recap, which you browse with « Previous » and « Next ».

## Shop and progression

**Can I buy Coins with real money?**
No. Coins are earned only on the portal: watching, requests, games, quests, achievements, levels. See [Progress, quests and games](portalab://wiki/guide-progression).

**Where are the quests?**
In the **Games** section of the menu, on the « Quests » page. « Today's games » also reminds you where you stand. See [Progress, quests and games](portalab://wiki/guide-progression).

**What happens if a booster gives me a look I already own?**
It is a duplicate: it gives you Coins back, depending on its rarity. The amount is shown on the booster's sheet. See [The shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique).

**I won a look but it is « Locked ». Why?**
It asks for a level or a Contribution you do not have yet. It is already yours and becomes usable as soon as you reach them, or right away with a level pass from « My reserve ».

**Do the items in my reserve expire?**
No. They add up and wait in « My reserve »: tap an item, then « Use now ».

**Can I get a refund for a purchase?**
Only if the product allows it: « Refund (until …) » then shows in « My purchases ». A declined special request or service access gives your Coins back.

**I missed a day: is my streak lost?**
Yes, it starts again from zero, unless you have a streak freeze: it covers the missed day automatically.

## Community

**If I fund a project that does not happen, do I lose my Coins?**
No. Your Coins are reserved, not spent right away, and given back in full if the project is rejected or abandoned. See [The community](portalab://wiki/guide-communaute).

**Can I appear in the leaderboards without showing my name?**
Yes. In your account, on the « My presence in the community » card, choose « Anonymous »: you stay ranked, without your name, your avatar or a link to your profile.

## Help, support and account

**Can the assistant see or do more than I can?**
No. It only sees and does what you can see and do. Any action it suggests, such as opening a ticket, waits until you tap « Confirm ». See [The assistant](portalab://wiki/guide-assistant).

**I get the message « You reached today's assistant limit ». What can I do?**
The assistant's quota resets the next day. If your portal offers them, the shop sells « Assistant capacity » products. Start a « New conversation » when you change topic: long conversations use up more.

**How can I tell whether a problem is already known before opening a ticket?**
Look at the Incidents page, or tap « Check first » in the ticket form: it lists the « Known incidents » and the wiki pages that may help. See [Support](portalab://wiki/guide-support).

**What does the « Waiting for you » state on my ticket mean?**
The team is waiting for an answer from you. Write it in « Your message » on the ticket's page, then tap « Send ».

**What is the difference between a ticket and feedback?**
A ticket is for getting help: it has a state and a conversation with the team. Feedback (the « Your feedback » page) is for remarks, ideas and bugs, followed from Sent → Read → Reply.

**How do I get or delete my data?**
In Privacy, « Request a copy of my data », then « Download my data ». To leave, « Delete my account » with your password: the deletion happens after a grace period, and you can cancel it by signing in again.

**Can I edit a wiki page?**
Only if your administrator gave you the right: you will then see « Edit » on the pages. Otherwise, report a mistake to support or through feedback. See [News, calendar and wiki](portalab://wiki/guide-actualites).
