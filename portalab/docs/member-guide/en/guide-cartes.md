Mediacards are the collectible cards of the portal's films and series: what a card is, its versions and frames, how to get cards, how to open a booster and how to complete your collections.

> [!TIP]
> The « Mediacards » guided tour, on the [Guided tours](/guide) page, shows you all of this in a minute.

> [!NOTE]
> Not a collector? Mediacards come with the « Collector » notch of the « How far do you want to play? » gauge (in the welcome screen, or in [My settings → Play](/settings/play)). At « Just the media » or « Passive », they are hidden: the menu, the titles' pages, the shop and the reserve no longer show them, and watching no longer gives you a Mediacards booster. The looks boosters and the shop stay; at « Passive », so does your progression.

## How it works

Every film and every series on the portal has its own **set**: a series of numbered cards, as in a trading card game. A set holds:

- the card of **the title** itself;
- the cards of **the people who made it**: directing (or creation, for a series), writing, music;
- the cards of the main **characters** (up to ten), with the actor or actress who plays them.

Each set has a three-letter code (the initials of its title), and each card has a collection number that never changes. When a title arrives on the portal, its set appears. If a title leaves the portal, the cards you have from it stay yours.

## Reading a card

![A character card and its six parts, numbered](shot:cartes-anatomie)

1. **Its name**, at the top.
2. **The title's genre**, which also gives the card its colour: « Sci-fi », « Action », « Fantasy », « Family », « Drama » or « Dark ».
3. **The artwork**: the character cut out over a scene from the title (or the title itself, or the person).
4. **The banner**: what the card is (« Character », « Film », « Director »…) and, on the right, the symbol of its edition.
5. **The title**: its poster, who plays the part, its year, and its figures, like the attacks on a card: « Audience rating », « Votes », « Box office » or « Seasons ».
6. **Its number** in the set and **its rarity**.

The card's border is the metal of its rarity.

> [!NOTE]
> In [Appearance](/settings/appearance), « Show the actor's portrait in a badge by their character, on the cards » adds the actor's photo in a small circle on character cards.

## Rarities

There are four rarities: **Common**, **Rare**, **Epic** and **Legendary**. The better known the title and the bigger the part, the rarer the card: the lead role in a huge hit is rarer than a supporting role in a little-known film.

Each title is compared with the titles **of its own country**: a lead role in a French hit is measured against French cinema, not against Hollywood. So every country has its legendaries. About 60% of cards are common, 28% rare, 9.5% epic and 2.5% legendary. Rarities are worked out again when the library changes.

## Versions, frames and editions

The same copy can come out in a special version:

- **Normal**.
- **Foil**: a holographic film that follows your pointer or your finger. About one card in eight, whatever its rarity.
- **Borderless**: the artwork covers the whole card. Only for a title's card, never common, and the rarer the card, the more often it happens.
- **… edition**: the card in the frame of its title's era; the rarer the card, the more often it happens.
- **… frame** (for example « Western frame »): the card in the frame of its title's **main genre**. There are 18 genre frames: « Action », « Adventure », « Animation », « Comedy », « Crime », « Documentary », « Drama », « Family », « Fantasy », « History », « Horror », « Music », « Mystery », « Romance », « Science fiction », « Thriller », « War » and « Western ». Here too, the rarer the card, the more often it happens.
- **… edition HD**: for legendaries only, and already foil.
- **Borderless foil**, **… edition foil** and **… frame foil**: the most sought after.

Some boosters also have their own **signature frame** (see « The shop's boosters » below).

## Getting cards

Cards come in **boosters**: sealed packs whose cards are drawn at random, according to their rarity.

- **By watching**: depending on your portal's rules, a film watched to the end or a finished season gives you a booster of that title. It only holds cards from that title (three cards, never the same one twice while others are left). A film, or a season of a series, gives you one only once, and the number of boosters you can receive per day is limited. A film stopped in the last two minutes before its end credits counts as watched to the end (see [Progress, quests and games](portalab://wiki/guide-progression)).
- **In the shop**: the « Boosters » department, « Mediacards boosters » shelf, offers boosters of 3 to 10 cards (see below). Once bought, the sealed booster waits for you. It cannot be refunded. See [The shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique).

![Mediacards boosters in the shop's « Boosters » department](shot:cartes-boutique)

### The shop's boosters

Depending on your portal, the shop offers a whole range:

- **genre boosters** (« Western booster », « Horror booster »…): titles of one main genre, with that genre's frame more often;
- **edition boosters** (« VHS edition booster »…): titles from one era, with its edition's frame more often;
- **rarity boosters** (« Rare booster », « Epic booster », « Legendary booster »): one card of that rarity guaranteed;
- **theme boosters**, for example « Japan », « Anime », « Kaiju and robots » or « Samurai and ninjas ».

A booster can have its **signature frame**, the frame of its own scene: about one card in four, among those that come out in the basic frame, takes it and keeps it (« … frame » in its versions). In a rarity booster, the guaranteed card is the one that can take it.

Your boosters waiting to be opened are in [Mediacards](/cards), « To open » view, and in [My reserve](/marketplace/reserve). Identical boosters are stacked in a single pile: a badge shows how many you have.

![The « To open » view: a booster (1) and the badge of a pile of identical boosters (2)](shot:cartes-a-ouvrir)

## Opening a booster

1. Tap the pack: it opens on its scene. Every booster has its own (some play a video, others reveal the cards full screen); a booster you bought opens on its product's scene, as in the shop.
2. Tear it open, then tap each card to turn it over. « Reveal all » turns them all over. The rarest comes last.
3. The summary shows how many new cards joined your collection. « My collection » takes you there.

In a pile, the pack opened is the first one; the badge counts those left.

> [!NOTE]
> A card you already have is not lost: it is one more copy, counted in your duplicates. Cards cannot be sold back for Coins.

## My Mediacards

[Mediacards](/cards), in the **Games** section of the menu, is built around your collection.

![The My Mediacards page, its five numbered markers](shot:cartes-mes-cartes)

1. **My collection**: the different cards you have out of all the portal's cards (and the percentage), the sets you have started and your copies.
2. **The three views**: « My collection », « To open » (your boosters, with their number on the tab) and « Every card ».
3. **The search** « Find a film, a series, an actor, a character… » finds sets, cards and people.
4. **« My sets »** keeps only the sets where you have at least one card; **« All sets »** shows them all.
5. **A set**: the title's poster, its code and how far along you are. Tap it to open its binder.

The « Every card » view shows every card on the portal, which you can filter by « Kind », « Rarity » and « Country », or with « Mine only »: handy for seeing which ones you are missing.

## A set's binder

Tap a set to open its binder: the cards you have are in colour, the others greyed out. A « ×2 », « ×3 »… shows your copies.

![A set's binder: its title and how far along you are (1), a card you have (2), a card still to find (3)](shot:cartes-classeur)

Tap a card to see it large with **all its versions**, the ones you have in colour, with how many. For a person, « All of …'s cards » shows their cards from every title they appear in.

## The collection on a title's page

On a film's or a series' page, the « Card collection » section shows its cards in a fan.

![The card collection on a title's page, its six numbered markers](shot:cartes-fiche-titre)

1. **The fan** turns on its own. Swipe it with your finger; tap the front card to open it.
2. **The arrows** move to the previous or next card.
3. **How far along you are**: the percentage of the set you have.
4. **By rarity**: your cards of each rarity, out of all those in the set.
5. **Your copies**, your duplicates and your foil cards.
6. **« See the whole collection »** opens the set's binder.

## Card achievements

Depending on your portal, achievements reward collectors: opening your first booster, opening 25, drawing a legendary, completing the collection of a film or a series, then those of several series or of ten titles. See [Progression](portalab://wiki/guide-progression).
