Spend the portal's currency (Coins): choose a product, buy it, open boosters and use your reserve.

## The portal's currency

- The shop works with the portal's currency. Its name depends on your portal: by default, it is **Coins**.
- Coins are earned only on the portal (watching, requests, quests, achievements, levels…), **never with real money**. See [Progress, quests and games](portalab://wiki/guide-progression).
- Your balance shows in the header and on [My progression](/progression).

## Find a product

Open the **Marketplace** section of the menu: its tabs are « Storefront », « My purchases » and « My reserve ». The home of the [storefront](/marketplace) puts forward what may interest you.

![The shop's home: the balance, the search, the departments, the spotlight and the picks, numbered](shot:boutique-accueil)

1. **Your balance** and the number of items « within reach ». Tap that number to see them.
2. **The search**: « Search products ».
3. **The departments**: **Interfaces**, **Profile**, **Notifications & effects**, **Features**, **Packs** and **Boosters**. An open department shows its shelves.
4. **Featured**: the products put forward, one at a time.
5. **The picks**: « On sale », « Within reach », « Nearly sold out », « Looks boosters to open », « Mediacards boosters » and « Free ». « See all » shows the whole pick.

![A shop department: its shelves, the sorting, the filters and a product, numbered](shot:boutique-rayon)

1. **The shelves** of the department, with their number of products. « Whole department » brings them together.
2. **The sorting**: « Recommended », by price, by rarity or by name.
3. **The rarity**: « Common », « Rare », « Epic » or « Legendary ».
4. **The filters**: « Within reach », « Not mine yet », « On sale ».
5. **A product**: its rarity, its type, its price, or « Owned ».

Tap a product to open its sheet on the side (full screen on a phone): you keep your place in the department. The sheet tells you what you get, the price, the copies left, the minimum level or Contribution, the purchase limit, whether it can be refunded and the conditions (« met » or « not met »). During a campaign, some products are on sale: the percentage shows on the product and the « Usual price » stays shown.

> [!TIP]
> The « See in the shop » links on other pages (Appearance, profile, assistant…) open the right type of product directly. « All types » then shows the whole department.

## Buy a product

![A product's sheet: what it tells you (1), « Try it » (2) and « Buy » (3)](shot:boutique-fiche)

1. Open the product's sheet. Its box (1) shows the price, the copies, the refund and the conditions.
2. For a theme, a palette, an ambience, a celebration or a notification style, tap « Try it » (2): it applies to the portal without paying anything. « Stop trying » removes it.
3. Tap « Buy · … Coins » (3) (or « Get it for free »).
4. Check the price and the refund, then tap « Confirm purchase ».

If you cannot buy it, the button tells you why: « Already in your collection », « Sold out: no copy left » or « Conditions not met ».

To wear a look you already own, tap « Equip » on its sheet. Themes, palettes and effects can also be chosen in [Appearance](/settings/appearance).

## Useful products

Besides looks, the shop offers (depending on your portal):

- **Request quota**: media requests on top of your quota. See [Request a title](portalab://wiki/guide-demandes).
- **Rescue**: saves a title that the library cleanup was about to delete, with « Save this title » on its page. See [Titles leaving soon](portalab://wiki/guide-bientot-supprime).
- **Streak freeze**: a missed day is covered automatically, and your streak goes on.
- **XP boost**: more XP for a while, within the portal's limit. Bought while another boost is running, it starts when that one ends.
- **Special request**: a request handled by hand by the administrators. You fill in a short form after buying it, in « My reserve ».
- **Service access**: an administrator approves the access and your account is created.
- **Project launch**: your idea becomes a proposal funded by the members. See [The community](portalab://wiki/guide-communaute).
- Extra **widgets**, **widget options** and **statistics**.
- **Assistant capacity** (more tokens, more reasoning…). See [The assistant](portalab://wiki/guide-assistant).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A special request or a service access can be declined. Your Coins are then given back.

## Open a booster

A booster is a sealed pack of cards drawn at random.

1. In the « Boosters » department, « Looks boosters » shelf, open a booster's sheet. Look at the number of cards, the guaranteed rarity and the « Odds per rarity ».
2. Tap « Open — … ». The price is paid when you open it.
3. Tap the pack, then each card to turn it over. « Reveal all » shows them all.

What you draw:
- **a new look** is yours;
- **a duplicate** gives you Coins back, depending on its rarity (the amount is shown on the sheet);
- **an item to keep** goes to your reserve.

Good to know:
- Some boosters can only be opened a limited number of times. Seasonal boosters are on sale only during their season.
- A look above your level is yours, but « Locked » until you reach the level or Contribution it asks for.
- « My latest openings », at the bottom of the « Boosters » department, lists your recent draws.
- The « Mediacards boosters », on their own shelf in the same department, give film and series cards for your collection: genre, edition, rarity or theme boosters, depending on your portal. Each one opens in its product's scene. See [The Mediacards](portalab://wiki/guide-cartes).

## My reserve

[My reserve](/marketplace/reserve) keeps « The items won in boosters, to use whenever you want. They never expire. » What you have several copies of adds up (« ×2 », « ×3 »…).

The page is sorted by what each item is waiting for. At the top, a bar leads to each part, with its number.

![My reserve: the bar of parts and their number (1), an item of the reserve (2)](shot:boutique-reserve)

1. **The bar**: tap a part to go to it.
2. **An item**: its picture, its number and its name. Tap it to open its window.

The parts:

- **« To complete »**: purchases waiting for your choices, for example a project launch or a special request. Tap one: its form opens in a window (« Complete “…” »). For a project launch, enter the title, the pitch and the goal, then « Launch the project ».
- **« To open »**: your sealed boosters, given to you (a level or achievement reward) or bought, and your Mediacards boosters. Tap one: it opens in its scene, at no cost. Identical boosters are grouped in one pile.
- **« To use »**: your items (extra requests, streak freezes, boosts, rescues, level passes…). Tap one to see what it does, then « Use now ». It takes effect as if you had bought it. If a form must be filled in afterwards, it waits for you in « To complete ».
- **« Waiting for their level »**: your locked looks, with the level they need (« waits for level … »).

**Level pass**: tap it, choose a look that is waiting and tap « Unlock ». You can wear it right away.

In the menu, a badge on « My reserve » shows what arrived since your last visit, or a purchase waiting for its form. Opening the reserve clears the new items.

## My purchases

[My purchases](/marketplace/owned) brings together « What you bought or were given, what waits for you, and your requests. »

- **At the top**, your figures: « In your collection », « Coins spent » and « To complete ». If a purchase is waiting for its form, « Complete » takes you to [My reserve](/marketplace/reserve).
- **My requests**: « Waiting », « Being handled », « Done » or « Declined — Coins given back ».
- **My collection**: everything you own, with a search and a filter by department.
- **Refund**: when the product allows it, « Refund (until …) » stays available until the date shown.

> [!WARNING]
> Packs, boosters, boosts, streak freezes, rescues, quotas, special requests, service access and project launches cannot be refunded. The sheet says so before you buy.
