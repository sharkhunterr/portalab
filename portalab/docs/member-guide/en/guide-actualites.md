How to stay informed: the team's news, your personal feed, the newsletters, the calendar of what is coming, and the wiki, where you find guides and answers.

In the menu, the **News** section brings together « Headlines », « My feed » and « Calendar ». The wiki is in the **Help** section.

## Reading the news

The [Headlines](/news) page gathers « What's new on the portal, straight from the team. »

![The Headlines page and its three numbered markers](shot:actualites-liste)

1. At the top, the **« Featured »** articles: the first one large, the next ones beside it.
2. The **categories** bar: each category has its colour, which its articles carry too. Click a category to see just that topic. « All categories » brings back the full list.
3. Below, the **« Latest news »**, each as a card with its category and date.

A dot marks an article you have not read yet. It goes away as soon as you open the article. In the menu, a badge on « Headlines » counts the articles you have not read yet.

Further down, **« On the web »** brings in the latest news from the sites the team follows for the portal.

Open an article to read it in full: its category, its date and its reading time (« 3 min read », for example) are shown at the top. « All news » takes you back to the list.

> [!NOTE]
> If an article does not exist in your language, the portal tells you: « This article is not translated into your language yet. »

Depending on what the team chose, a new article can also reach you as a notification (« New article: … »). You can add the « Latest news » or « Featured news » widget to your [home page](portalab://wiki/guide-accueil).

## My feed

[My feed](/feed) is your own page: « Everything that comes, mixed: the server and each source, in its colour. » Nobody else sees what you follow there.

![My feed: what is coming soon (1), the filters (2), an entry and its source (3), something new (4)](shot:actualites-fil)

1. **« Coming soon »**: what is announced for the people you follow (an upcoming film with an actress, for example), and whether it is already « Requested » or « Available ».
2. **The filters**: « All », « For me », then one chip per source, each in its colour. Tap a source to see only that one; tap it again to see everything.
3. **An entry**, grouped by day (« Today », « Yesterday »…): its source, its colour, when it arrived.
4. **« New »** marks what arrived since your last visit.

What your feed mixes:

- **« On the server »**: what just arrived in the library for what you follow (« New film », « New series », new episodes…);
- **the team's sources**: sites, RSS feeds, YouTube channels, podcasts, chosen by the administrator;
- **your own sources**, if you add some (see below).

**« For me »** keeps only what concerns what you follow: an article about a person or a series you follow is also labelled « For me ».

A YouTube video plays **right there**: tap its picture to start it, without leaving the portal. Other articles open on their own site, in a new tab. When the feed is long, it is split into pages (« Previous page », « Next page »).

In the menu, a badge on « My feed » counts what arrived since your last visit. Opening the feed resets it.

### Choosing what you follow

At the bottom of the feed, expand **« Manage my feed: what I follow, my sources »**:

- **« What I follow »**: the people, series and genres you follow. The « Stop following … » cross removes one. « Follow a genre » adds one.
- To follow a person (an actor, a director, an author…) or a series, tap **« Follow »** on their page.
- **« My sources »**: your own RSS or Atom feeds (a blog, a YouTube channel, a forum…). Paste its address into « A feed's or a site's address », then tap « Follow ». Their articles only show in your feed. « Import an OPML file » and « Export (OPML) » bring in or back up a list of feeds.
- **« Suggested sources »**, above the feed: the sources your portal recommends (sites, YouTube channels, podcasts…). Tap one to follow it in a click; it then leaves the list.

A source you have just added only counts as new what it publishes from then on: its older articles are in the feed, but not in the unread badge.

> [!TIP]
> The **« My feed »** widget shows the latest news from your sources on your [home page](portalab://wiki/guide-accueil): articles, videos, podcasts. YouTube videos play right there too. « All my feed » opens the page.

## Newsletters

The portal can write **automatic newsletters** by itself, at regular intervals (every day, every week or every month), from what happens on the portal: titles that arrived in the library, recent releases, upcoming releases… Each one has its own layout.

Depending on what the team chose, a newsletter:

- **appears in the news**, like an ordinary article of its channel (its category in « Headlines »), with its colour;
- and/or **goes out by e-mail** to its subscribers, in the portal's look.

You are subscribed to each newsletter by e-mail until you unsubscribe, and you can unsubscribe **from one without the others**. At the bottom of each e-mail, « Unsubscribe from this newsletter » opens your preferences, right where its switch is. The details are in [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications).

## The calendar

The [calendar](/calendar) shows, day by day, what is coming on the portal. Depending on your portal, you find there:

- the **releases** of the films, series and books you follow;
- the community's **events** and the **campaigns**;
- **maintenance** and **incidents**;
- published **news**.

Each item has an icon and the name of its category, for example « Films », « Series », « Events », « Maintenance ».

### Changing the view

![The calendar and its five numbered markers](shot:actualites-calendrier)

1. Move forward or back with the « Previous » and « Next » arrows. « Today » brings you back to the current day.
2. The month or week shown.
3. Pick a view: « Month », « Week » or « List ».
4. The categories also work as filters: click one to hide it or show it again. « Show all » brings them all back.
5. In the « Month » and « Week » views, click a day to see its details. When you arrive, today's is already open: what is happening today shows without a click.

Times follow your time zone and the first day of the week chosen in « Language & region ».

Click an item's title to open its page, when it has one (the title's page, the incident, the article…).

### Getting a reminder

For an upcoming item, you can ask for a reminder:

1. In the day's details or in the « List » view, find the item.
2. In « Remind me », choose how long before (the lengths offered depend on your portal).
3. Click « Remind me ».

The reminder arrives as a notification (« Coming up: … »). If the item's time changes, your reminder follows and you are told. If the item is removed, the reminder is cancelled and you get a notification too. Your reminders are listed at the bottom of the page, in « My reminders », with their state (« scheduled », « sent »…).

> [!TIP]
> If a source does not answer, the calendar says so and still shows the other items.

The « Calendar » or « Upcoming » widget can also show all this on your home page.

## The wiki

The [wiki](/wiki), in the Help section of the menu, holds « Guides, how-tos and answers, written by the community. » This member guide is part of it.

### Finding a page

- The wiki's home shows the main pages as cards, with their sub-pages.
- The « Find a page » field filters pages by their title.
- You can also search the whole wiki with the header search (**Ctrl K**). See [First steps](portalab://wiki/guide-premiers-pas).

### Reading a page

A wiki page shows:

![A wiki page and its four numbered markers](shot:actualites-wiki-page)

1. the « Browse the wiki » tree on the side, to go from one page to another (below the content on a phone);
2. the page's location (its path in the wiki) at the top;
3. « Contents », which opens the list of the pages in the same guide;
4. « On this page », the outline of its headings, to jump straight to a part.

At the bottom of the page, « Previous » and « Next » lead to the neighbouring pages.

You only see the pages you are allowed to read.

### Writing in the wiki

Members read the wiki. You can only write in it if the administrator gave you the right to. In that case:

- « New page » creates a page: give it a « Title » and a « Page content »;
- « Edit », at the top of a page, opens it for editing;
- « Save » keeps your changes;
- « History » shows the page's versions: « Compare with previous » shows what was added or removed, and « Restore this version » goes back to an older version.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If someone saved the page while you were editing it, the portal shows their changes. Work them into your text, click « I have merged the changes », then save again.

Without that right, the « Edit » and « History » buttons do not show on pages. Spotted a mistake? Report it to [support](/support) or through [feedback](/feedback). See [Support](portalab://wiki/guide-support).
