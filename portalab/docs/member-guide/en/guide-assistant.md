Ask the portal's assistant your questions, find out what it can look up and do for you, and read its answers.

> [!NOTE]
> The assistant carries the name your portal gave it (for example « Nova »); that name shows at the top of its window. It only exists if the administrator turned it on. If it is unavailable, it tells you: « The assistant is unavailable right now. Everything else keeps working. »

## Opening the assistant

You can open it in several ways:

- **The round button** with sparkles, in a corner of the screen, on every page (« Ask the assistant »).
- **The header search**: type your question, then choose « Ask the assistant: “…” ».
- **The search page**: the « Assistant analysis » block answers your search; « Continue as a conversation » opens the assistant's window.
- **A title's page**: in a request's journey, or when a title is about to leave the library, the « Ask the assistant » button asks the question for you (for example « Where does « … » stand, and why is it not available yet? »).
- **A new ticket**: « Ask the assistant » runs a diagnosis before the ticket is opened (see [Support](portalab://wiki/guide-support)).

On a computer, the window opens in the middle of the screen. On a phone, it takes up the whole screen.

![The assistant's window and its six numbered markers](shot:assistant-fenetre)

1. **The gauge** of your use for the day (see « Your daily use »).
2. **« Conversations »**: your past conversations.
3. **« New conversation »**.
4. **« On this page: … »**: the page you are asking from.
5. **Question ideas**, to tap.
6. **The « Ask the assistant… » field** and the « Send » button.

## Asking a question

1. Write your question in the « Ask the assistant… » field.
2. Press **Enter** or « Send ». **Shift + Enter** starts a new line.
3. The answer is written as it comes. While it looks things up, the assistant shows « Looking it up in the portal… ».
4. To interrupt it, tap « Stop ».

At the start of a conversation, question ideas are offered as buttons, for example « Are all the services working? », « Where do my support tickets stand? », « What are the latest announcements? » or « Which of my requests are still on their way? ». Your portal may offer others.

### « On this page »

At the top of the conversation, the « On this page: … » line shows the page you are asking from. That way, « this title » or « this ticket » really means what you have in front of you. Remember to open the right page before you ask.

## What the assistant can do for you

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The assistant only sees and does what **you** can see and do. It never has more rights than you.

Depending on what your portal has turned on, it can look up:

- **Media**: search for a film, a series, a book or a game in the catalogue; your recent history; the titles recommended for you; where a title stands (requested, released, downloading, imported…) and whether it is about to leave the library.
- **Support**: your tickets and their state; a ticket's details and its latest replies.
- **The portal's status**: the state of the services, current or recent incidents, ongoing and planned maintenance.
- **News**: the latest published announcements.
- **The wiki**: search the wiki and read a page, if its writers opened it to the assistant; the help for the page you are on.
- **Your progression**: your level, your XP, your Contribution and your Coins balance.
- **The statistics** you are allowed to see.
- **The shop**: the products and their prices.
- **The portal's search** and the **useful pages** to open for what you need.

If it cannot find the answer, it tells you. It can then give you the link to a wiki page or offer to open a ticket.

## Actions to confirm

When the assistant offers to act for you, for example to **open a support ticket**, a « Confirm this action » card appears under its answer.

- It sums up exactly what will be done.
- « The assistant proposed this action. Nothing happens until you confirm. »
- Tap « Confirm » or « Decline ».
- The card expires after a while (« Expires in … seconds »). If it shows « Expired — ask the assistant again. », simply ask again.

## Reading an answer

- **Sources**: the numbers in the text point to the « Sources » cards under the answer. Each card shows its kind (« Live data », « History » or « Knowledge base ») and its date. Tap a card to open the matching page.
- **Caveats**: if some data is incomplete, old or unavailable, a note says so above the sources.
- **No source**: the line « Assistant interpretation — not backed by a cited source. » flags an answer you should check.
- **Suggested pages**: « Open … » buttons lead to useful pages. Depending on your portal, the suggested page may open on its own.

Under each message:

- « Copy » copies the text (without the source numbers).
- « Send again » sends your question again; « Ask again » asks for a new answer.
- « As expected » or « Not as expected »: give your opinion. For an answer that was not as expected, you can write « What did you expect? (optional) », then « Send my comment » or « Skip ».

> [!NOTE]
> The answers you rate, with their question, can be read by the administrators to improve the assistant.

## Your conversations

- « Conversations » (the clock icon) shows your past conversations, with their date. Tap one to pick it up again.
- « Delete conversation » (the bin) erases it.
- « New conversation » (the + button) starts from scratch, with the context of the current page.

Conversations are kept for a limited time, depending on your portal.

## Your daily use

The assistant has a quota per day and per member. The gauge at the top of the window shows the percentage already used. Tap it to see « Today's use »:

- « Used », « Day's allowance » and the time it « Starts again ».
- « Reserve »: the recharges you own, used once the day's quota is used up.
- « Capacities bought »: what you got on top, and until when.

Each question, and the data the assistant reads to answer it, counts. Long conversations count more: start a new conversation when you change the subject.

If you see « You reached today's assistant limit. It resets tomorrow. », wait until the next day. If your portal offers it, the « Get more in the shop. » link leads to the « Assistant capacity » products: more tokens each day, recharges, a longer memory, a stronger model, more reflection or extra tools. See [The shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique).

> [!TIP]
> « You are sending messages too quickly. Wait a moment. »: wait a few seconds before sending your message again.
