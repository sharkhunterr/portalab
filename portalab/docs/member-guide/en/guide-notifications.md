How the portal lets you know: the bell and its notifications, the on-screen messages, the e-mails and the newsletters, and how to set it all up.

## The bell

In the header, the **bell** shows the number of unread notifications (up to « 99+ »). On a phone, it can also be in the bottom bar, under the name « Notifications ».

A notification tells you, for example, that a title you requested has arrived, that someone replied to you, that a reward is waiting for you or that a maintenance is coming up.

## Reading your notifications

1. Click the bell: the « Notifications » panel opens over the page (full screen on a phone).
2. Notifications are sorted from newest to oldest, by day: « Today », « Yesterday », « This week », « Earlier ». Unread ones are marked.
3. Click a notification to open what it is about. It is then marked as read.

![The « Notifications » panel, its four numbered markers](shot:notifications-panneau)

1. The panel's tools: « Select », « Mark all as read », the « Notification preferences » and « Close ».
2. The tabs (see below).
3. A notification: tap it to open it.
4. Its « Mark as read », « Archive » and « Delete » buttons.

Some notifications carry a badge: « Alert » for what matters, and the rewards they brought you (XP, Coins…).

### The tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| « All » | All your notifications, except archived ones |
| « Unread » | Only the ones you have not read yet |
| « Alerts » | Alerts (outages, security, important messages) |
| « Archived » | The ones you put away |

At the bottom of the list, « More » loads older notifications.

### Tidying up your notifications

On each notification, small buttons let you:

- « Mark as read »;
- « Archive » (and, in the « Archived » tab, « Unarchive »);
- « Delete ».

At the top of the panel:

- « Mark all as read » marks everything at once;
- « Select » lets you pick several, then mark them as read, archive them or delete them together. « Select all » ticks everything; « Done » leaves the selection. To delete, the portal asks you to confirm.

> [!TIP]
> Archiving a notification takes it off the list without losing it: you find it again in « Archived ».

## On-screen messages

When a notification arrives while you are on the portal, a small message also shows on screen for a few moments. « Dismiss » makes it go away. A critical alert stays on screen until you click « Acknowledge ».

Even if you did not see it, the message always stays in the bell.

In [Appearance](/settings/appearance), you can choose where these on-screen messages appear, turn their sound on or off, and pick their style and animation. Some styles come from the [shop](/marketplace). See [My account and my settings](portalab://wiki/guide-compte).

## Choosing what you receive

Open « Notification preferences »: in [My settings](/settings) (personal menu, behind your picture), « Notifications »; from the bell's panel; or straight from [this page](/settings/notifications).

The kinds of notifications are grouped by theme, one card each: « Account & security », « Media », « Community », « Progression », « Shop », « News & announcements », « Support & services »… Each line has two switches:

- « In the portal »: the notification arrives in the bell;
- « By e-mail »: you also receive it by e-mail.

Every change is saved at once (« Preferences saved. »).

The « My feed: what arrives for what I follow » line sets up the new items in [My feed](/feed): in the bell (that is the starting setting), by e-mail as well, or not at all.

![Notification preferences, one card per theme](shot:notifications-preferences)

1. The card's theme.
2. The two columns « In the portal » and « By e-mail ».
3. A locked line: its padlock and « Set by your administrator ».

> [!NOTE]
> A line marked with a padlock and « Set by your administrator » cannot be changed. Some notifications are required, for example for your account's security: their switch stays on.

### The e-mail digest

In the « E-mail digest » card, choose how you receive your e-mails:

- « Send each e-mail »: one e-mail per notification;
- « Daily digest »: a single e-mail a day that gathers everything;
- « Weekly digest »: a single e-mail a week.

![The « E-mail digest » card and its choice](shot:notifications-resume)

## Newsletters

The portal can publish newsletters, for example the titles that arrived in the library, recent releases or upcoming releases. **Automatic newsletters** write themselves, every day, every week or every month. They appear in the [news](/news) and can also be sent by e-mail. See [News, calendar and wiki](portalab://wiki/guide-actualites).

You are subscribed to each newsletter by e-mail until you unsubscribe. To choose:

1. Open the [Notification preferences](/settings/notifications).
2. In the « Newsletters by e-mail » card, find the newsletter:
   - « Automatic newsletters »: each automatic newsletter, with when it goes out (for example « every Monday at 9:00 »);
   - « Channels »: the news channels the team sends by e-mail.
3. Turn its switch on or off. Each newsletter is set separately: you can keep one and leave the others.

Turning a channel off also stops the automatic newsletters that appear in it: their line says « Off with its channel « … » ».

At the bottom of each newsletter e-mail, « Unsubscribe from this newsletter » takes you straight to this card. Newsletters can always be read in the news, even if you no longer get them by e-mail.

> [!NOTE]
> This card only shows if your portal sends newsletters by e-mail.

## Common questions

- **I don't get any e-mails.** Check that the « By e-mail » switch is on for the notifications you want, and check your « E-mail digest » choice.
- **A notification no longer interests me.** Turn its line off in the preferences, unless it is set by the administrator.
- **I want to be told about a calendar event.** Use « Remind me » in the [calendar](/calendar): the reminder arrives as a notification. See [News, calendar and wiki](portalab://wiki/guide-actualites).
