Ask for help with a ticket, follow your requests, give the team your feedback and find out whether a problem is already known.

In the menu, everything is in the **Help** section: « Guide » (the guided tours), « Wiki », « Support » (your tickets), « Feedback », « Service status » and « Incidents ».

## Before opening a ticket

A service is not working? First check whether it is a known problem:

- The [Incidents](/incidents) page shows the portal's state: the incidents in progress, the ones resolved recently and the planned maintenance. Filter with « All », « Ongoing » or « Resolved ». Open an incident to see its « Timeline » and the « Affected services ». « See the services' state » leads to the state of each service.
- An « Ongoing incident » or « Planned maintenance » banner may also show at the top of the portal.

If the team is already working on the problem, there is no need to open a ticket. See also [Control center](portalab://wiki/guide-centre-de-controle).

## Open a ticket

Open [My tickets](/support), then tap « Open a ticket ». You land on [the form](/support/new).

![The form of a new ticket, with its six numbered markers](shot:support-nouveau)

1. Fill in the « Title ».
2. Choose the « Kind of request ». The kinds offered depend on your portal (a problem, a question, an access request…). Then answer the questions that come with the kind you chose: the required questions must be filled in.
3. If it concerns a specific service, choose it in « Service (optional) ».
4. Write your message in « Describe the problem ».
5. If you wish, tap « Check first » or « Ask the assistant » (see below).
6. Tap « Open the ticket ».

The ticket then opens on its own page. If something is missing, the form tells you: « Fill in: … ».

> [!TIP]
> A problem with a specific title (subtitles, language, a cut-off file…)? Use **« Report a problem »** on its page instead: the ticket is tied to the title, and the title's page shows it.

### Check first

After describing the problem, tap « Check first »:

- « Known incidents » lists the ongoing incidents that might match. If one of them is yours, tick « My problem is this incident »: your ticket will be attached to it.
- « These pages may help » suggests wiki pages that may already answer your question.

### Get help from the assistant

If the assistant is turned on, « Ask the assistant » runs a diagnosis: it looks at who you are, the ongoing incidents, the service's state and the wiki pages, then makes a suggestion. Each step tells you whether it is « Real data » or an « Assistant interpretation ».

It can also prefill the ticket. The message « The assistant prefilled the form; check it before sending. » then shows. **Nothing is sent without you**: read it over, correct it, then tap « Open the ticket ». See [The assistant](portalab://wiki/guide-assistant).

> [!TIP]
> You can also create a ticket from the « My tickets » widget on your home page, with « New ticket ».

## Follow your tickets

[My tickets](/support) shows one card per ticket: its number (T-…), its title, its state, its priority and its last update.

- Filter with « In progress », « Done » or « All ».
- Tap a ticket to open its page.

The possible states:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| « Open » | Your ticket has arrived; the team has not picked it up yet. |
| « In progress » | The team is working on it. |
| « Waiting for you » | The team needs an answer from you. |
| « Resolved » | The team considers the problem fixed. |
| « Closed » | The ticket is over. |
| « Cancelled » | The ticket was dropped. |

Depending on your portal, the team may use other states.

## A ticket's page

- **At the top**: the number, the state, the priority and, if there is one, the linked incident.
- **« Details »**: your description and your answers to the questions.
- **« Conversation »**: the messages between you (« You ») and the « Support team ». Write in « Your message », then tap « Send ».
- **« Attachments »**: tap « Attach a file » to add a screenshot or a document. The size and the types of files accepted depend on your portal. A refused file shows « This file was refused (type, size or check). ».
- **« History »**: everything that happened on the ticket (creation, state changes, replies…).
- **« Ticket actions »**: the buttons allowed for you in the current state. For example: reply when the team is waiting for you, cancel a ticket in progress, close a resolved ticket, or reopen it if the problem comes back. Reopening is only possible for a certain time after the resolution, depending on your portal.

You get a notification when the team replies (« New reply on ticket T-… ») or when the state changes. See [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications).

## Give your feedback

The [Your feedback](/feedback) page (« Feedback » in the menu, or « Send feedback » from My tickets) is where you tell the team what you like, what is missing or what is not working.

![The Your feedback page: a request from the team (1), the kinds of feedback (2) and the follow-up of your feedback (3)](shot:support-avis)

1. **« Your opinion is asked »**: the questions the team asks every member.
2. **« Give my opinion »**: choose what it is about to write to the team.
3. **« My feedback »**: what you sent and where it stands.

### Your opinion is asked

When the team asks every member some questions, the « Your opinion is asked » block shows at the top. Each request shows its number of questions and its deadline (« until … »).

1. Tap « Answer ».
2. Answer the questions (text, choices, a star rating…).
3. Tap « Send ».

You can answer each request only once: « You already answered this request. »

### Give my opinion

Under « Give my opinion », choose what it is about. By default, three kinds of feedback are offered:

- **General feedback**: an impression, a compliment, a remark, with a rating.
- **A bug**: something does not work as expected.
- **An idea**: an idea to improve the portal.

Your portal may offer others. Fill in the short form, then tap « Send ». The message « Thank you — the team will read it. » confirms it was sent. « Cancel » closes the form.

> [!TIP]
> A bug that blocks you? A ticket is better: the team answers you in the ticket and you can follow its state. Feedback is meant more for remarks and ideas.

### Follow my feedback

The « My feedback » section lists what you sent. For each piece of feedback, a tracker shows where it stands:

1. « Sent »: your feedback is on its way.
2. « Read »: the team has read it. At this step, you may see « Taken up » (the team did something with it, for example a ticket or a proposal) or « Closed ».
3. « Reply »: the team has answered you. Its « The team's reply » shows under your message.

Filter with « All », « Waiting » or « Answered ». A notification lets you know: « The team answered your feedback ».
