Why some titles are about to leave the library, where to spot them, and how to save one with a rescue bought in the shop.

## Why titles are deleted

To keep some space free, the library's cleanup regularly removes titles. It follows rules set by your administrator, specific to your portal. The page of a title at risk tells you which rule picked it.

For a series, sometimes only a few seasons go, not the whole series.

> [!NOTE]
> If your portal does not use the library cleanup, none of this appears.

## Spotting the titles about to leave

- On the **Media** page, the **« Leaving soon »** row shows the titles on the deletion list, with their departure date when it is known.
- On your home page, add the **« Leaving soon »** widget: it lists the titles the cleanup is about to delete, the first to go first (see [The home page and its widgets](portalab://wiki/guide-accueil)).
- On a **title's page**, the **« Leaving soon »** box gives you all the details.

![The « Leaving soon » row on the Media page, each poster marked « leaving soon »](shot:supprime-rangee)

## Reading the « Leaving soon » box

On the title's page, the box tells you:

- **when** the title leaves: « This title will be deleted from the library in 5 days. »;
- for a series, **how many seasons** go: « 2 seasons will be deleted from the library in 5 days. »;
- sometimes no date is known yet: « This title is on the library's deletion list. »;
- **why**: the rule that picked it, shown as « Rule: … »;
- **how many rescues** you have: « No rescue », « 1 rescue left »…

![The « Leaving soon » box on a film's page, with no rescue: « Buy a rescue »](shot:supprime-encadre)

## Saving a title

A **rescue** takes a title off the deletion list.

1. Open the title's page.
2. In the « Leaving soon » box, click **« Save this title »**.
3. The message « Title saved: it will not be deleted. » confirms the rescue.

What you need to know:

- **One rescue per title.** For a series, it also saves all its seasons that were about to go.
- **For everyone.** The title stays in the library for all members, not just for you.
- **For good.** Once saved, no cleanup rule picks it up again.
- **Once used, it is not given back.** A spent rescue cannot be recovered.

After the rescue, the page shows « You saved this title…: it will not be deleted. » or, if someone else did it, « A member saved this title…: it will not be deleted. »

> [!WARNING]
> If the message « The rescue did not go through. Try again later. » appears, the library cleanup did not answer. Your rescue has not been used: try again a little later.

## Getting rescues

If you have no rescue, the box offers **« Buy a rescue »**. This button opens the shop on the **« Rescue »** products, paid for with the portal's currency (Coins).

- A product can contain one or more rescues, depending on your portal.
- Your rescues stay available until you use them. There is no rush: keep one in reserve for the day a title you love is at risk.
- A rescue can also come out of a booster. It is then stored in **« My reserve »**: click **« Use »**, then **« Use now »**, to add it to your rescues.

To learn more about purchases, boosters and the reserve, see [The shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique).

## Asking the assistant

If the assistant is available on your portal, the **« Ask the assistant »** button in the « Leaving soon » box asks the question for you: why this title is on the deletion list, when it leaves, and how to save it. See [The assistant](portalab://wiki/guide-assistant).
