Manage your profile, choose how the portal looks, set your language and region, control what others see of you and link your media accounts.

All these settings are gathered in [My settings](/settings), in your personal menu (behind your picture, under **Me**). The page is organised by what you want to do: « Profile », « Appearance », « Language & region », « Notifications », « Linked accounts », « Privacy » and « Play ».

- On a computer, the list of pages stays to the left of the open page.
- On a phone, the list is the page: tap a topic to open it, then « My settings » to come back.
- « Search a setting » finds a setting by its name or by a word close to it (« password », « dark », « time zone », « e-mail »…).

## My profile

The [Account](/account) page shows your profile as others see it, and how to change it.

### Changing my picture

1. Tap the image icon on your avatar (« Change my picture »).
2. Choose an image: PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, 2 MiB at most.
3. To take it off, tap « Remove my picture ».

### My name and « About me »

In the « Profile » card:

- « Display name »: the name other members see. If it is already taken, choose another one.
- « About me »: a few words about yourself.
- « Who can see “About me” »: « Everyone », « Portal members », « Leaderboards only » or « Only me ».

Tap « Save ».

### Customising my profile

On your profile, « Customise » opens « Customise my profile »: « Banner », « Picture frame », « Film backdrop », « Name style », « Title », « Badge showcase » (up to three badges) and « Level badge ». Only the items you own are offered; « See in the shop » shows the others. See [Community](portalab://wiki/guide-communaute) and [The shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique).

### My presence in the community

This card sets what other members see of you: the leaderboards, the recent activity and the members' directory. It does not depend on your play level: it applies at every notch of the gauge, even « Just the media ». The welcome asks it on a screen of its own, « How do you want to appear to the others? ». Either way, nothing is shown outside the portal.

- « Public »: the portal's members, and only them, see your name and your avatar in the leaderboards, the activity and the directory.
- « Anonymous »: you stay in the community without being recognised. In the directory, you are counted as « Anonymous », at the end of the list, and a search for your name does not find you. What you do stays in the activity, signed « An anonymous member ». In the leaderboards, your place and your points still count, under « Anonymous ». Your name, your avatar and a link to your profile appear nowhere.

« Finer settings » opens the privacy center. If « An administrator set this. » shows, you cannot change it.

The « Shortcuts » lead to « My public profile », « My progression », « Appearance » and « Privacy & my data ».

## How the portal looks

The [Appearance](/settings/appearance) page only changes the display: never your data.

### Choosing an interface

The **Interfaces** block shows each interface with a preview. An interface changes the colours and the fonts, but also the layout, the navigation (side menu, top bar, dock…), the animations and the sounds.

1. Tap an interface's card: it applies straight away.
2. Choose **light** mode, **dark** mode or **your device's**.

Only the interfaces you own are offered. A line shows how many more are available in the shop, with « See the shop ». A checkbox also lets you show or hide the campaigns' seasonal interfaces.

### Colours, badges and effects

![The « Colour palette » block: the palettes (1) and the line to the shop (2)](shot:compte-couleurs)

- « Colour palette »: the accent colours laid over your theme. « The theme's » keeps the original colours.
- « Level badge »: the look of the level badge everyone sees. « Classic » changes with your level.
- « Level-up »: how a new level is celebrated. « Try it » shows it.
- « Ambience and celebrations »: the « Ambient animation » behind the portal (snow, falling code, fireflies…) and the « Reward celebration ». « Follow the theme » keeps your interface's own; « Preview » and « Try it » show them before you choose. The « Live weather » ambience follows the real sky of a place: « Change the place », « Search a town or a city » or « Use my position ».
- « Notifications »: the « Style » and the « Animation » of the notification bubbles, and their position on the screen. See [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications).
- Some of the portal's extensions have their own block too (« Brought by …: choose the look it wears. »).

Some items are won or bought in the shop. An item marked « Won, and waiting for you » unlocks at a given level or Contribution.

### Reading comfort

The display block sets how the portal reads for you:

- the **density** (compact, comfortable or spacious);
- the **text size**, with a slider;
- **high contrast**;
- **reduced motion** (follow your device, on or off);
- « Decorative animations »: « All », « Discreet (saves battery) » or « None (still images) »;
- the portal's **sounds**.

> [!NOTE]
> A greyed-out setting has been set by the administrator. Your own choice is kept and comes back if it is released. The administrator can also reduce motion for everyone.

### Safe mode

If the display causes trouble, « Use safe mode » shows the basic interface, with no theme and no added effects. It only applies to this browser and stays on until you leave it. You can also turn it on by adding ?safe=1 at the end of any page's address. A banner reminds you of it; « Leave safe mode » brings everything back to normal.

## Language and region

On [Language & region](/settings/language), each choice is independent and is saved straight away:

- « Interface language »: the language of the whole portal.
- « Formats (dates, numbers) »: how dates and numbers are written.
- « Time zone ».
- « Clock »: « 12-hour » or « 24-hour ».
- « First day of the week ».

« Automatic » lets the portal decide from your browser. The « Preview: … » line shows the result.

> [!TIP]
> If your language has been turned off, the portal says so: your choice is kept and will apply again as soon as the language is back.

## Privacy and data

The [privacy center](/privacy) shows what the portal keeps about you, who can see it and how long it is kept.

- At the top, a summary counts your information visible to everyone, to the portal's members, only in the leaderboards, or to you alone.
- The information is sorted by part of the portal (profile, community, media, activity, progression, statistics, shop, support). Each item shows what it is and how long it is kept.
- When it is allowed, choose who sees it in the list next to it. A padlock marks a setting fixed by the administrator: your choice is kept and will come back later.

### Your data

- « Terms of use »: the version you accepted, and « Read the terms ».
- « My data »: tap « Request a copy of my data ». The copy is prepared; « Check again » updates its status. When it is ready, tap « Download my data ».
- « Delete my account »: confirm with your password, then tap « Delete my account ».

> [!WARNING]
> The deletion happens after a grace period, depending on your portal. During that period, you can cancel it simply by signing in again. The portal shows the deadline: « Deletion scheduled. You can cancel until … ».

## Media accounts

On [Media accounts](/settings/media-accounts), link your accounts on the media services so that your activity (what you watch, read or listen to) shows in the portal.

1. Under « Link an account », choose the « Service ».
2. Enter your « Account on that service » (your name or your account number on that service).
3. Tap « Link account ».

The link shows in « Linked accounts » with the status « Waiting for verification ». **Nothing is credited to you until the link is verified.** If the service allows it, enter the « Verification code » and tap « Verify ». Otherwise, an administrator approves the link. Once approved, it turns to « Verified ».

To remove a link, tap the bin (« Remove »). See also [Watch, read, listen](portalab://wiki/guide-medias).

## Play

The [Play](/settings/play) page brings back the « How far do you want to play? » gauge from the welcome screen; change your mind whenever you like. Slide the cursor: each notch adds to the one before, and your choice is saved straight away.

| Notch | What it brings |
|---|---|
| « Just the media » | Watch, read and request, without the game: no cards, no games, no quests and no community in your menu. |
| « Passive » | XP and levels for what you watch, read or request; quests, achievements, leaderboards. |
| « Collector » | On top, the Mediacards: boosters as you watch, a collection to complete. |
| « Keen » | On top, the day's mini-games, the week's challenges and games with others. |

Below « Collector », the Mediacards leave the menu, the titles' pages, the shop and the reserve, and watching no longer gives a Mediacards booster. Looks boosters stay. In the menu, « Today's games » and « Play together » only show at « Keen », and « Quests » leaves at « Just the media », together with the « Community » section and your achievements. Public or anonymous is not set here, but in the « My presence in the community » card (see above).

« Set who sees what more finely, in privacy » opens the privacy center.

The « This profile's dashboard » card offers the dashboard the team prepared for your play level. « Take my profile's dashboard » replaces your current layout with it; « Dashboard updated » confirms the change.
