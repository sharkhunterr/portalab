When your server has a **RomM** game library, its games can be played **right inside Portalab**: open a game's page and tap **« Play here »**. The game starts in RomM's emulator (EmulatorJS): it plays just as it does in RomM, without leaving the portal or signing in to RomM.

## Playing

- **« Play here »** appears on a game's page when the portal can run it — the same consoles as RomM: NES, Super Nintendo, Game Boy, GBA, Mega Drive, Master System, PlayStation, Nintendo 64, DS, PSP, arcade, Commodore, Amstrad, Atari and many more, plus **Flash** games (with Ruffle) — and the game library has its file. **« Play on RomM »** stays next to it.
- The game opens in full screen. Tap **« Play »**: the page shows each loading step — the emulator, the console's core, its BIOS, the game, your save — with its progress, then the game begins; if something fails, it tells you why. The emulator's bar (at the bottom) gives you its controls, full screen, save states, the gamepad and, on a phone, the touch gamepad.
- The arrow at the top left closes the game and takes you back to its page.
- A few consoles (DOS, PSP, 3DS) need a page isolated from other sites; the game's page is. If such a game does not start, reload the page.

## Controls

- Keyboard, **gamepad** (plugged in or over Bluetooth) or, on a phone, the **touch gamepad** — laid out like the console's own for Sega consoles.
- The emulator's controls and options are changed in its menu (bottom bar, *Controls* and *Settings*): they are kept **per console**, with your profile, and follow you to your other devices.

## Saves

Your progress follows you (your profile, in a household) on all your devices:

- **The in-game save** (the one the game makes itself, as on the console) is kept every minute while you play and when you leave, then loaded again the next time you play.
- **Save states**: open **Saves** (the floppy disk at the top right while you play) and tap **« Save here »** on one of the 5 slots — it keeps a picture of the screen — then **« Load »** whenever you like. The emulator's *Save State* / *Load State* buttons use the last slot you chose.
- **Resuming**: leaving the game keeps your progress (« Where you left off », with a picture of the screen). The game's page always shows **your saves** (picture, slot, date — tapping one opens the game straight on it), with **« Resume »** (the game then asks which one) and **« New game »** (the in-game save stays). When the game library has several versions of the game (regions, languages, revisions), a new game asks which one to start, and each version keeps its own saves.
- Each save is also **copied to RomM**, tagged « Portalab » and with your name, so you can find it in RomM. One member's or one profile's saves are never another's.
- A save state holds the emulator's whole memory: for the bigger consoles, it may go over what the portal accepts (10 MB unless your administrator changed it).

## Your own RomM account

By default, the portal writes your play sessions, saves and states to the RomM account it uses itself (shared, each save tagged with its owner). To have them on **your own** RomM account:

1. Open *My settings › Media accounts*, the **« My game library account »** card, and tap **« Authorise Portalab »** — the home page also offers it, as soon as your administrator has matched you with your RomM account.
2. A code appears with **« Approve in RomM »**: open it (signed in to RomM with your account) and approve the code.
3. The card says **« Your games go to your account … »**: your play sessions, saves and states now go there (those of a household profile go to its master's account, tagged with the profile).

The portal only asks RomM for what it needs (who you are, your play sessions, saves and states, its device) and keeps the authorisation encrypted. **« Remove the authorisation »** revokes it (in RomM too); revoking it in RomM works as well: the portal then goes back to the shared account and offers to authorise again.

## What counts

Play time counts like a film watched in the portal's player: in your **statistics** (a game played in Portalab), your **XP** and your **streak** according to the earning rules, and the **screen time** of a household profile. Only the time the game is running, with the page in the foreground, counts: a tab left in the background does not.

Flash games keep their progress in the browser (no save states).

Each play session is also reported to **RomM**, which keeps the play time of the account the portal uses there. That account's RomM history leaves out the portal's sessions (they are already counted).

## Household profiles

A profile plays by its master account's rules: a forbidden game (by its title, or all games) does not open, and once the day's **screen time** is used up, the game stops with a message. See « My household and its profiles ».
