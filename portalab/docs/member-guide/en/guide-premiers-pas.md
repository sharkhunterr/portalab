Your first steps on the portal: signing in, reading the header, finding your way around the menu, searching for anything and starting the guided tours.

## Signing in

1. Open your portal's address.
2. Enter your username and your password, then confirm.
3. If your portal offers single sign-on, a button at the top of the form lets you sign in with that account instead.

Good to know:

- After several failed attempts, the portal asks you to wait a few seconds before trying again.
- If your administrator sent you an **activation code**, the activation page asks for that code and a password of your choice (at least 12 characters).
- If the portal does not answer, a « The portal is unavailable for a moment » page appears. It keeps retrying on its own, and your page comes back as soon as the portal answers.
- The other "in-between" pages look just as calm: « This page is not here », « This page is not open to you », a service that is briefly missing, an error, or loading. Each one offers « Back to the home page » or « Previous page »: you never hit a dead end.

### The terms of use

If your portal has published terms, it asks you to read and accept its **terms of use** before going any further. The « Accept and continue » button lets you in. « Refuse » signs you out: the portal stays closed until you accept. When the terms change, the portal shows you « What changes » and asks you to accept the new version.

## Welcoming new members

On your first visit, a few welcome screens open (« Step 1 of … »: how many depends on your account):

- **Welcome**: what you will do on the portal (watch and read, play if you want to, set everything up the way you like). « Let's go » opens the next screen.
- **A face and a name**: this is how the other members will see you; you also choose your « Language » here, each with its flag. It applies right away, including to the rest of the welcome.
- **Your PIN** (for a household profile only): 4 to 6 digits, entered twice, so that no one else in the household can open your profile. You can skip this screen and choose one later from your avatar's menu.
- **Your media accounts**: link your Plex, Jellyfin… account; what you watch there then counts in your statistics and your progression. Choose the « Service », enter your « Account on that service » and tap « Link account ». If the service gave you a « Verification code », enter it and tap « Verify ». You can link more accounts later, in « Media accounts » (see [My account and my settings](portalab://wiki/guide-compte)). A household profile does not get this screen: it uses the account holder's accounts.
- **A household?** (for an account holder, never for a profile): do other people share your account? « Make my household » goes on to « How your household earns », « Your family » and « Each profile's rules »; « No profiles » skips them. See [My household and its profiles](portalab://wiki/guide-foyer).
- **How far do you want to play?**: a gauge with four notches. Drag the slider: each notch adds to the one before.

  | Notch | What it brings |
  |---|---|
  | « Just the media » | Watch, read and request, without the game: no cards, games, quests or community in your menu. |
  | « Passive » | The game plays itself: XP and levels from what you watch, read or request, plus quests, achievements and leaderboards. |
  | « Collector » | On top of that, the Mediacards: boosters as you watch, a collection to complete. |
  | « Keen » (recommended) | The whole game: on top of that, the daily mini-games, the weekly challenges and multiplayer games. You never have to play: everything is simply within reach. |

  Below « Collector », the Mediacards are hidden: not in the menu, not on title pages, not in the shop, and no more Mediacards boosters as you watch. The other boosters stay. In the menu, « Today's games » and « Play together » only appear at « Keen », « Mediacards » from « Collector » up, and « Quests » from « Passive » up. At « Just the media », the « Community » section and your achievements also leave the menu.
- **How do you want to appear to the others?**: your privacy in the community, on a screen of its own, separate from the play gauge (it applies to every notch, even « Just the media »).

  | Choice | What the other members see |
  |---|---|
  | « Public » | Your name and avatar in the members' directory; what you do (achievements, levels, contributions) in the community's activity; your place in the leaderboards, with your name. |
  | « Anonymous » | In the directory, you count as « Anonymous », with no name, avatar or profile; what you do stays in the activity, signed « An anonymous member »; in the leaderboards, your place and your points count, as « Anonymous ». |

  Either way, nothing is shown outside the portal. The choice shown at first is your current setting; it is saved with « Next ». « Set who sees what more finely » opens the privacy center.
- **Your portal, to your taste**: say what you like, and your home is built around it.
  - « What are you here for? »: films, series, books, audiobooks, magazines, manga, comics, retro video games. The portal uses this to put forward what you like.
  - « On your home »: « Resume and discover », « The latest releases », « News about my favourites », « My requests », « Play », « Statistics », « Service status », « The community », « The radio ». At first, « Resume and discover », « The latest releases », « News about my favourites », « My requests » and « Service status » are ticked, plus « Play » if you play. « Play » and « The community » are greyed out at « Just the media »: raise the gauge to add them.
  - « Make my home with these choices », ticked on your first visit: your dashboard starts from the one the team prepared for your play profile, keeps the widgets for what you ticked, adds the ones it is missing and leaves out those for what you did not tick. You can move everything afterwards. When you see these screens again, the box is unticked, unless you just changed notch on the gauge: ticking it replaces your current layout. At « Just the media », the home it builds has no game widget and no community widget.
- **How should we tell you?**: pick a ready-made setting, then fine-tune it theme by theme.
  - « Everything »: every piece of news, in the portal and by e-mail.
  - « The essentials » (recommended, and preselected on your first visit: it applies if you move on without changing anything): everything in the portal; by e-mail your account, your media and your tickets, in one daily digest.
  - « The minimum »: your account, your media and your tickets in the portal; by e-mail your account only, once a week.

  Each theme has two switches: « In the portal » and « By e-mail » (a household profile only has the portal: e-mails go to the account holder). Below, « E-mail digest » sets how often e-mails are sent (« Send each e-mail », « Daily digest », « Weekly digest ») and « Newsletters by e-mail » the news channels you receive. You can also set everything in detail in « My notifications » (see [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications)).
- **Your look**: light, dark or « Like my device », a color from the palettes on offer, and the « Text size » (100% to begin with).
- **How the portal works**: in a few words, the menu and the search, watching, reading and listening, playing, the wiki, help, the community, and what XP, coins and contribution bring you (for a profile with a shared pot: its earnings also go to the household).
- **All set**: everything stays customizable (the interface, the dashboard, notifications and e-mails, privacy), each setting one gesture away. « Take the tour » starts a one-minute tour; « Explore on my own » lets you discover things yourself.

You can skip any screen with « Skip », and change everything later: the play gauge and your profile's dashboard in [My settings → Play](/settings/play), public or anonymous in your account, on the « My presence in the community » card. You can see these screens again from the [Guided tours](/guide) page, with the « Welcome screens » button.

## The header

The header stays at the top of every page (or at the bottom, depending on your theme). From left to right, you will usually find:

![The portal's header and its six items, numbered from left to right](shot:premiers-entete)

| No. | Item | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The portal's name or logo | Back to the home page |
| 2 | The search bar | « Search or jump to… »: titles, pages, members, articles… |
| 3 | The Control Center | The portal's status at a glance (incidents, maintenance, announcements) |
| 4 | The bell | Your notifications, with the number of unread ones |
| 5 | Level and Coins | Your level, your XP bar and your balance in the portal's currency (Coins); a click opens « My progression » |
| 6 | Your picture | The **personal menu** |

The Control Center has its own help page: [Control center](portalab://wiki/guide-centre-de-controle). So do notifications: [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications).

> [!NOTE]
> The name of the currency depends on your portal. By default, it is called « Coins ».

### The personal menu

Click your picture to open the « Personal menu ». This is the portal's **Me** section:

- « Profile »: your profile, as the others see it;
- « My progression »: level, XP, quests and achievements;
- « Achievements »: all the portal's achievements and how far you have got;
- « My settings »: all your settings (account, appearance, language and region, notifications, linked accounts, privacy, play);
- « Sign out ».

These settings are described in detail in [My account and my settings](portalab://wiki/guide-compte).

## The menu and its sections

The menu gathers the whole portal into sections. Depending on your portal, your permissions and your play level, you will see:

| Section | Its pages |
|---|---|
| **Home** | your dashboard |
| **News** | « Headlines », « My feed », « Calendar » |
| **Media** | « Library », « Request a title », « My requests », « My taste » |
| **Games** | « Today's games », « Quests », « Play together », « Mediacards » (depending on your play level: see « How far do you want to play? » above) |
| **Community** | « Overview », « Members », « Leaderboards », « Events », « Proposals », « Projects » |
| **Statistics** | « My media », « My activity », « My requests », « Community » |
| **Marketplace** | « Storefront », « My purchases », « My reserve » |
| **Help** | « Guide », « Wiki », « Support », « Feedback », « Service status », « Incidents » |

Your personal pages (« My progression », « Achievements », « My settings ») are in the personal menu, behind your picture.

You only see the pages you have access to. A section opens on its first page; its other pages are **tabs**, at the top of the content, to switch from one to another. The breadcrumb above reminds you where you are; at the bottom of some pages, « Related » suggests nearby pages.

A **badge** next to a section or a page counts what is waiting for you: the « Headlines » articles you have not read, what is new in « My feed », what has arrived in « My reserve »…

![The menu and its sections (1), the breadcrumb (2) and the tabs of the News section (3)](shot:premiers-menu)

> [!TIP]
> Where the menu sits depends on your theme: a column on the side, a bar at the top, a panel that slides open… If you cannot find it, look for the « Open navigation » button (three lines).

### On a phone

The header keeps the essentials: home, search, the Control Center, your level, notifications and the menu. Your profile and sign-out are in the menu. With some themes, a bar at the bottom of the screen offers « Home », « Search », « Notifications » and « Menu ».

## Searching for anything

The search in the header answers as you type:

1. Click in « Search or jump to… », or press **Ctrl K** (⌘ K on a Mac) or **/** from any page.
2. Type a few letters. The portal pages that match come first, then the top results of each kind: titles, people, articles, wiki pages, tickets…
3. Use the arrow keys to move, **Enter** to open, **Esc** to close.
4. The last choice, « Search for “…” », opens the search page with all the results and filters.

Before you type, the list shows your « Recent searches » and « Advanced search »: the [search page](/search), with its filters. It is no longer in the menu, but always one click away from this field. Titles you can request also show up in the results: it is a handy way to [request a title](portalab://wiki/guide-demandes). If the assistant is available and you type a question, the search offers « Ask the assistant: “…” » (see [The assistant](portalab://wiki/guide-assistant)).

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| Ctrl K or / | Search |
| Ctrl Shift K | Open the command palette |
| ? | Show the keyboard shortcuts |
| g then h | Go to the home page |
| g then s | Go to the services |
| g then a | Go to your account |

Shortcuts do not fire while you are typing in a field, except Ctrl K.

## Guided tours

A guided tour highlights one part of the screen at a time, on the real pages, with a bubble explaining what it is for.

- « Next » moves on to the next step; on some steps, « Do it » does the action for you.
- The arrow keys move forward or back, and **Esc** leaves the tour.

All the tours are on the [Guided tours](/guide) page (« Guide », in the Help section), grouped by theme: « Getting started », « Media », « Play and share », « Help ». For each one, you see how many steps it has, whether you have already seen it (« Seen ») and the reward it still brings. « Start » begins a tour, « See again » replays it. The first one, « The portal in one minute », shows the level, the search, notifications, navigation and the dashboard.

![The Guided tours page: the welcome screens (1), the first tour (2) and a tour from one theme (3)](shot:premiers-visites)

### First steps on the dashboard

On the home page, the « First steps » card offers small missions (take the tour, customize your profile, request a title…). Each one opens its tour and earns you XP and Coins, depending on your portal. « Put away the first steps » hides the card; the tours stay on the [Guided tours](/guide) page.

To go further, see [The home page and its widgets](portalab://wiki/guide-accueil).
