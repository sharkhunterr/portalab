Your statistics: what you have watched, listened to and read, your habits, your activity on the portal, your requests in numbers, and the recap that sums up a past period.

The **Statistics** section of the menu brings together four pages:

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [My media](/media/insights) | What you have watched, listened to and read: your time, your titles, your habits |
| [My activity](/statistics/me) | Your figures on the portal, chosen by your administrator |
| [My requests](/media/requests/stats) | Your title requests in numbers |
| [Community](/statistics) | The figures of the community and the portal |

## My media

Open [My media](/media/insights) in the « Statistics » section. You can also get there through **« My statistics »**, on the Media page.

### Choosing what you look at

![My media: the period (1), Me or Whole server (2), the category (3), the tabs (4) and the four figures (5)](shot:statistiques-medias)

1. **« Period »**: « Last 7 days », « Last 30 days », « Last 3 months », « This year », « Last 12 months », « All time », or a specific year.
2. **« Me »** or **« Whole server »**: your figures, or the activity of the whole community. The server view is anonymous: no member is named in it. In a household, **« My household »** shows the whole household's figures, each profile's included (see [My household and its profiles](portalab://wiki/guide-foyer)).
3. **The category**: « All », « Films », « Series », « Live TV », « Music », « Books », « Audiobooks », « Radio », « Video games » or « Requests ». « All » also counts the time spent playing.
4. **The tabs**: « Overview », then the category's own tabs (see below).
5. **The four figures** of the overview.

### What you will find

On the **« Overview »** tab:

- four figures: **« Time spent »**, **« Plays »**, **« Titles »** and **« Active days »**, compared with the previous period;
- **« Activity »**: a curve over time, by kind. The **« Show »** menu switches from time spent to the number of plays;
- **« When »**: the days and hours you watch;
- **« Most watched »**, with their posters;
- **« Genres »**, **« Actors and artists »**, **« Decades »**;
- **« Records »**: « Busiest day », « Longest session », « Best streak », « Titles finished »;
- **« Box office »**: the combined budgets and revenue of the films you have watched;
- **« Devices »** and **« Latest plays »**.

For films and series, more tabs go further: **« Casting »**, **« Production »**, **« Playback »** (quality, stream type, players and platforms) and **« Profile »** (your genre profile, period after period). The other categories have their own tabs too, depending on the kind.

The **« Requests »** category shows the figures of your requests, like the « My requests » page below.

### Statistics to unlock

Depending on your portal, some cards, some tabs, the long periods or the « Whole server » view are unlocked in the shop, with the portal's currency (Coins). A locked card shows a padlock, the message « This statistic unlocks in the shop. » and the **« See in the shop »** button. In the period menu, a period you have to unlock is marked « in the shop ».

![A locked statistic and its « See in the shop » button](shot:statistiques-verrou)

> [!NOTE]
> Nothing showing? Your statistics appear as soon as one of the library's services reports your plays **and** your accounts are linked. Check your [media accounts](/settings/media-accounts) (see [Watch, read, listen](portalab://wiki/guide-medias)).

## My requests in numbers

[My requests](/media/requests/stats), in the « Statistics » section, shows your title requests over the chosen **« Period »**: where they stand, **« My requests' status »**, **« What became of them »**, **« My rank as a requester »** (anonymous), **« Requests over time »**, **« Time to availability »**, the most watched titles among those requested, and the genres you requested compared with the genres you watched. Some of these cards are unlocked in the shop, depending on your portal.

The list of your requests itself stays in the Media section. See [Request a title](portalab://wiki/guide-demandes).

## My activity and the community

Two pages show the statistics your administrator has set up:

- [My activity](/statistics/me): yours, for example your sign-ins or what you have finished;
- [Community](/statistics): those of the community and the portal.

They vary from one portal to another and work the same way:

- Choose the **« Period »**: « Last 7 days », « Last 30 days », « This week », « This month » or « This year ».
- Single figures come first, in small cards; the charts follow.
- Each card shows its value as text and its trend compared with the previous period.
- **« Show the data table »** shows the figures of a chart as a table.
- **« Hide »** hides a card you are not interested in. It moves to **« Hidden statistics »**: click **« Show »** to bring it back.

A card can be **locked**. It then tells you how to unlock it: earn an achievement, reach a level, own an item from the shop, or wait for a campaign. See [Progression](portalab://wiki/guide-progression).

> [!TIP]
> « Not enough participants yet to show this statistic privately »: a statistic about the community only appears once enough people take part in it. That way, nobody can guess what one particular member is doing.

## Your recap

The recap sums up a finished period (a month, a quarter or a year, depending on your portal), statistic by statistic.

1. When your recap is ready, a notification lets you know: « Your … recap is ready ».
2. Click it: the recap opens on the « Your recap: … – … » page.
3. Move from one statistic to the next with **« Previous »** and **« Next »**. Nothing scrolls on its own: you go at your own pace.

Your recap is personal: only you can see it. If no recap is ready yet, the page shows « No recap yet. ». Recap notifications are set in the « Recaps » category of your preferences (see [Notifications](portalab://wiki/guide-notifications)).

## Your statistics on the home page

You can add your statistics to your home page:

- the **« My statistics »** widget: time spent, plays and most watched titles, with the period, category and display of your choice;
- the **« Statistic »** widget: one of the portal's statistics.

See [The home page and its widgets](portalab://wiki/guide-accueil).
