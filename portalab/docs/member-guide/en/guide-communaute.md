Discover the members, customise your profile, follow the activity and the leaderboards, join events, and propose or fund projects.

## Finding your way in the community

Open the **Community** section of the menu. Its pages are tabs, at the top of the content: « Overview », « Members », « Leaderboards », « Events », « Proposals » and « Projects ».

The [Overview](/community) tab gives you the big picture:
- the « Leaderboard » podium and your place;
- « My quests » and the « Campaigns running »;
- the « Latest activity »;
- the « New members », the « Proposals needing support » and the « Recent achievements ».

Quests are in the **Games** section, and your achievements in your personal menu (« Achievements »). Both are explained in [Progress, quests and games](portalab://wiki/guide-progression).

## Find a member

1. Open the [Members](/community/members) tab.
2. Type a name in « Search members ».
3. Tap a member to open their profile.

A profile can show « About », their progression, their achievements, their recent activity and their projects. Each member chooses what they show, so some sections may be empty or hidden.

## Customise your profile

On your own profile, tap « Customise ». The « Customise my profile » window has several tabs:

- « Banner »: the top of your profile;
- « Picture frame »: an outline around your picture;
- « Film backdrop »: the image of a film or series from the library as your banner, once unlocked in the shop;
- « Name style »: a font and an effect for your name;
- « Title »: shown under your name;
- « Badge showcase »: up to three badges next to your name;
- « Level badge ».

Some designs are free. The others come from [the shop](portalab://wiki/guide-boutique) or from boosters; « See in the shop » takes you there. An item you won but that is still locked shows the level or Contribution you need to reach.

Your frame and your name style follow you: other members see them in the leaderboards and in the activity.

## Follow the activity

The activity feed shows what happens, day by day: a level reached, an achievement unlocked, a quest completed, a project proposed. Open it from « Latest activity », with « See all ».

A secret achievement shows as « a secret achievement ». A member who chose to be anonymous shows as « An anonymous member ».

## Check the leaderboards

![The tabs of the Community section (1), « Ranking by » (2) and the period (3)](shot:communaute-classements)

1. Open the [Leaderboards](/community/leaderboards) tab.
2. Choose what to rank in « Ranking by »: « XP », « Level », « Contribution », « Achievements » and, if your portal offers it, « Coins ».
3. Choose the « Period »: « This week », « This month », « Last 6 months », « This year », « Last 3 years » or « All time ».

The leaderboard shows the podium, your position and the places won or lost « Since yesterday ». Over a period, only what you earned during that period counts. Levels are always ranked all time.

> [!NOTE]
> Leaderboards are recalculated regularly, not after every gain: the date of the last update is shown (« Updated … »).

> [!TIP]
> Prefer to stay low-key? In your account, « My presence in the community » lets you choose « Anonymous ». You stay in the directory, the activity and the leaderboards (your place counts), but as « Anonymous »: without your name, your avatar or a link to your profile. See [My account and my settings](portalab://wiki/guide-compte).

## Events

The [Events](/community/events) tab lists the upcoming events meant for you, with the campaigns running at the top. They also show in the calendar. See [News, calendar and wiki](portalab://wiki/guide-actualites).

## Propose an idea

In the [Proposals](/community/proposals) tab:

1. Tap « Propose a project or feature ».
2. Choose the « Kind »: « Project » or « Feature ».
3. Give a title and a description.
4. Tap « Submit the proposal ».

Depending on your portal, the proposal is published right away or waits for a moderator's approval. You will find it in « My proposals ».

On an open proposal:
- tap « Support » to vote (« Withdraw my support » to cancel);
- tap « Take part » and choose a role: « Contributor », « Helper » or « Tester ».

If you are the author, you can « Ask for help », « Withdraw the proposal » and « Recognize this contribution » of a participant. A recognised contribution can earn the participant Contribution, depending on your portal.

## Fund a community project

The [Projects](/projects) tab (« Community projects ») brings together the members' ideas, funded together in Coins.

![The community projects: the figures, the stages and a project, numbered](shot:communaute-projets)

1. **The figures**: the projects open for funding, being built and released, the Coins gathered and your contributions.
2. **The stages**, to filter the projects, with the number of projects in each.
3. **A project**: its state, its step (« Step … of … »), the Coins gathered towards its goal, its backers and, if it is open, « Contribute ». « New » marks a project added, or that moved forward, since your last visit.

- Filter by stage: « All », « Proposed », « To fund », « Being built », « Released » or « Closed ».
- A project goes through several states: « Idea », « Proposal », « Open for votes and funding », « In validation », « In development », « In beta », then « Released ».
- In the menu, a badge on « Projects » points out the projects that are new or moved forward since your last visit.

### A project's page

Tap a project to open its page:

- **what has been gathered**, its bar towards the goal and what is left: « … Coins left to gather », or « Goal reached! »;
- the **« Backers »**, with what each one gave and since when. An anonymous member shows as « A member »;
- the **« Lifecycle »**: a horizontal timeline, from the idea to the release, showing the step the project is at;
- the « Project notes » and, if it comes from a proposal, « See the proposal ».

To fund an open project:
1. On its page, go to « Contribute ».
2. Pick one of the « Quick amounts », or enter the « Coins to reserve », within the limits shown.
3. Tick « I understand that funding does not guarantee the project will be built. »
4. Tap « Reserve Coins ».

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your Coins are **reserved**, not spent right away. They are used when the project is released or at certain milestones, as its « Funding rules » explain. If the project is rejected or abandoned, they are refunded to you in full.

« My fundings », at the bottom of the projects page, shows for each project your Coins reserved, used and refunded. You are notified when a project you fund changes state.

### Launch your own project

At the bottom of the projects page, « A feature idea? » offers **« Launch a project »**: it takes you to the « Project launch » product in the shop. After buying it, open [My reserve](/marketplace/reserve): the project waits for you there, in « To complete ». Tap it: its form opens in a window. Enter the « Project title », the pitch and the « Goal to reach » (the administrators confirm it or adjust it), then tap « Launch the project ». Your idea opens as a proposal, and anyone can fund it.
