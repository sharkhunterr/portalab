# Quickstart — Community
1. As different viewers open a member's profile: identity/progression/achievements sections appear only per the member's visibility; a Private section is simply absent; no friends/follow/DM/like controls exist anywhere.
2. Unlock an achievement as a Community-visible member → feed shows it (localized); switch visibility to Private → it disappears at once; Private actors never enter the feed.
3. Open leaderboards: ranking comes from 013 with opt-out honored; hidden achievements stay hidden until unlocked; a challenge shows as a time-boxed quest.
4. Propose a project → `pending_moderation`; a moderator approves → visible and votable; vote as several active accounts (no minimum level, free); retract and re-cast; a suspended account is refused; counts are public, voters are not.
5. Fund the project via the Marketplace flow (015) — voting and funding stay independent; status changes arrive as `project.status_changed` and notifications.
6. Participate as a tester; the proposer recognizes it once → `community.contribution_recognized` → Contribution granted by 013 (self-recognition and repeated pairs refused).
7. Search for a member/proposal → only permitted results; empty result offers a next action.
